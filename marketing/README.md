# Just One KISS Marketing Campaign Laboratory

**Classification:** `GEN` / GENERAL / NOT TIMELINE-SPECIFIC
**Status:** Internal working laboratory; no item is approved for publication merely because it appears here.
**Purpose:** Put the project's campaign knowledge, planning tools, experiments, production briefs, and release controls in one usable workspace without creating a competing source of truth.

## What this laboratory is

This folder is a portable, self-contained marketing system. It includes its own controlled brand snapshot, expression layer, five campaign lenses, production records, social masters, and runnable landing page. It can be copied to another repository without a runtime or content dependency on its former parent repository.

It is **standalone for campaign work**: a marketer can begin here, understand the brand, select an audience lens and funnel job, build a brief, create a production record, preview the campaign site, and run release review. Historical source paths are retained only as provenance in `source_library/`; they are not dependencies. The portable authority for new work is [`canon/BRAND_CANON.md`](canon/BRAND_CANON.md) and its paired JSON.

## Start here

1. Read [Laboratory charter](LAB_CHARTER.md) for authority, status, and working rules.
2. Read the portable [Brand Canon](canon/BRAND_CANON.md) before writing or designing.
3. Use the [Marketing Expression Layer](expression/MARKETING_EXPRESSION_LAYER.md) to trace truth through measurement.
4. Choose one of the [five campaign lenses](campaigns/README.md) and a funnel job.
5. Build the campaign in [Campaign architecture](strategy/CAMPAIGN_ARCHITECTURE.md) and choose placements in [Channel playbook](strategy/CHANNEL_PLAYBOOK.md).
6. Plan copy and assets with [Content and asset system](production/CONTENT_AND_ASSET_SYSTEM.md).
7. Follow [Production workflow](operations/PRODUCTION_AND_RELEASE.md) and [Measurement plan](operations/MEASUREMENT_AND_EXPERIMENTATION.md).
8. Create records from [`templates/`](templates/) and maintain the live ledgers in [`registers/`](registers/).
9. Consult [Source catalog](source_library/SOURCE_CATALOG.md) when tracing a decision or locating legacy production material.

## The operating sentence

> Select one audience problem, lead with one marketing family, make one action unmistakable, prove every claim, and publish only the exact reviewed release.

## Current campaign model

The existing first-run plan uses five funnel jobs: **awareness, interest, consideration, conversion without purchase, and retention/nurture**. The new SSOT adds nine expressive families. They are different dimensions:

- **Funnel stage** says what must change in the audience.
- **Marketing family** says which emotional and visual argument will create that change.
- **Channel/placement** says how little or how much can be communicated.
- **Release record** says exactly what was approved and when it must stop.

Do not mechanically assign one family to one stage. Use the recommended defaults, then test alternatives without changing facts, rights posture, or safety meaning.

## Hard boundaries

- This laboratory does not approve facts, claims, likenesses, trademarks, music, footage, fan media, effects, accessibility promises, or official affiliation.
- “Fire” is normally color and energy language, not a claim of literal flame or pyrotechnics.
- The current event is free only while the controlled current source confirms it; never use paid-ticket or artificial scarcity language for a free event.
- Exact public copy comes from the current language SSOT at production time.
- Platform specifications and advertising policies change; verify them before export or spend.
- Fan submission is not blanket permission for advertising, merchandise, or sublicensing.
- Warnings, consent, directions, and critical facts remain plain and legible.
