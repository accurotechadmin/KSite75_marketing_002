## Shared stage definitions

1. **Awareness:** create distinctive recognition and the correct emotional category.
2. **Consideration:** explain difference with observable, rights-cleared proof.
3. **Interaction:** earn a low-risk action without pretending it is a sale.
4. **Conversion:** fulfill the campaign objective—signup, direction action, or qualified inquiry—without fabricated urgency.
5. **Post-conversion / WOM:** provide value, request permission, and turn authentic experience into reusable evidence.

## Feedback loop

Stage 5 testimony, artifacts, questions, and outcomes can feed Stage 1–3 only after rights, privacy, factual, and accessibility review. Record the source release and permission in the new proof record.
