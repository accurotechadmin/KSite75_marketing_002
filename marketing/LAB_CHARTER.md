# Marketing Laboratory Charter and Authority Model

## 1. Mission

The laboratory exists to convert controlled Just One KISS identity into traceable campaign releases. It welcomes exploration but separates ideas from claims, drafts from approved copy, and attractive assets from cleared assets.

## 2. Authority

Use this precedence for campaign decisions:

1. portable brand authority in `canon/BRAND_CANON.md` and paired JSON;
2. the hierarchy and controls in this charter and `README.md`;
3. the trace model in `expression/MARKETING_EXPRESSION_LAYER.md`;
4. campaign canon, audience, funnel, and channel guidance inside this folder;
5. approved campaign overlays and exact copy/fact records inside this folder;
6. active templates, site code, and cleared assets as implementation evidence;
7. historical paths in `source_library/` as provenance only, never runtime or claim authority;
8. draft registers as working destinations, not fact authority.

A lower layer may interpret a higher layer but may not silently alter it. When two sources conflict, pause the affected claim and use the more conservative rights, safety, privacy, or approval position.

## 3. Status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `idea` | Unscreened possibility; not ready for production |
| `draft` | Being written or designed; facts and rights may be unresolved |
| `needs_review` | Complete enough for named reviewers |
| `approved_internal` | Suitable for internal planning, not public release |
| `approved_public` | Approved content, still requiring a specific release record/export check |
| `scheduled` | Exact approved release assigned to channel and time |
| `live` | Published and monitored |
| `held` | Publication blocked by a named unresolved condition |
| `expired` | No longer current; remove or replace |
| `archived` | Retained as evidence and learning, not for reuse without review |

No file in this folder starts above `draft` unless an authorized owner changes its record.

## 4. Separation of layers

- **Evergreen canon:** identity, personality, palette roles, type roles, visual devices, voice, independence, rights posture.
- **Option menu:** nine families, funnel patterns, audience modes, channel recipes.
- **Campaign overlay:** objective, segment, offer/fact set, date window, destinations, budget status.
- **Piece record:** one message, placement, format, asset/copy references, CTA, variants.
- **Release record:** immutable exact copy and exports, checksums, approvals, publish/expiry data.
- **Learning record:** delivery, attention, action, quality, safety/privacy incidents, conclusions.

## 5. Roles

At minimum assign: campaign owner, copy owner, design/asset owner, fact owner, rights reviewer, safety/accessibility reviewer, channel operator, final approver, and measurement owner. One person may hold several roles, but blank ownership is a hold.

## 6. Change rule

Change the most specific authoritative source first. Do not “fix” runtime copy in a campaign file. When approved truth changes, identify dependent pieces, update drafts, expire live releases when necessary, and retain an audit trail.

## 7. Definition of done

A campaign is not done when art exists. It is done when the intended audience/action are clear, files are accessible, all public facts are verified, rights and safety are resolved, destinations work, tracking is documented, exact exports are approved, publication is monitored, and expiry/archival is completed.
