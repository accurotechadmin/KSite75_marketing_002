# Audiences and Journeys

## Segment cards

### Devoted theatrical-rock fans

- **Need:** recognition, respect, spectacle, communal release.
- **Do not assume:** official status, detailed lore knowledge, or blanket acceptance of every effect.
- **Strong families:** Love Letter with Volume, Shadow to Fire, Join the Rally, Open the Road Case.
- **Proof:** original performer/craft, stage image, clear independence statement, real community content.

### Curious local visitors

- **Need:** a plain explanation of the event, why it is special, and whether attendance is practical.
- **Strong families:** Arena Arrives, One Night / Full Fire, Before the First Chord.
- **Proof:** exact event facts, venue/address, admission, warnings, directions, accessible contact.

### Families and sensitive guests

- **Need:** welcome without minimization of loud sound, bright lights, fog, or flashing patterns.
- **Strong families:** Before the First Chord, Join the Rally.
- **Proof:** visible advisories and contact route; never a universal “safe for everyone” promise.

### Social viewers and collectors

- **Need:** immediate visual reward, portable identity, and something worth saving or sharing.
- **Strong families:** Chrome-Blood Spectacle, Shadow to Fire, Open the Road Case.
- **Proof:** original macro detail, numbered series, cleared artifact, functional destination.

### Returning participants

- **Need:** new value after the first click or event interaction.
- **Strong families:** Open the Road Case, Join the Rally.
- **Proof:** actual updates, Q&A, tips, cleared media, or a dated next drop—not an empty shell.

### Venue buyers, press, and partners

- **Need:** audience promise plus credible preparation, usable media, and direct contact.
- **Strong families:** Built to Land and Light, Love Letter with Volume, Arena Arrives.
- **Proof:** public-safe package summary, approved photography, independent posture, named inquiry path.

## Journey-state model

| State | Audience thought | Campaign job | Evidence threshold | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unaware | “I have never heard of this.” | Create distinctive recognition | Identity + one visual promise | Notice/visit |
| Aware | “This looks interesting.” | Explain difference | Sensory/craft proof | Explore/watch |
| Evaluating | “Can I actually attend?” | Remove friction | Verified practical facts | Plan/get directions |
| Ready | “I want to stay connected or commit.” | Make the action low-risk | Exact value exchange | RSVP/join updates |
| Engaged | “What else is here?” | Reward and involve | Real content/community utility | Open/share/ask |
| Advocate | “Who else should know?” | Enable accurate sharing | Portable fact card + link | Tell crew/share |
| Buyer | “Will this fit my venue/audience?” | Establish qualification | Verified package + contact | Inquire |

## Message hierarchy by audience

Every piece should contain only what its audience needs:

1. **Recognition:** identity and central promise.
2. **Relevance:** why this audience should care.
3. **Proof:** one credible image, fact, artifact, or capability.
4. **Qualification:** only the practical caveat necessary now.
5. **Action:** one clear next step.

Do not make cold audiences read logistics dossiers or make committed visitors hunt through mythology for an address.
