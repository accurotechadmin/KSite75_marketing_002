# Channel and Format Playbook

Platform specifications, policies, and product names are volatile. Values in legacy briefs are planning references only; confirm current requirements before production or spend.

| Channel/use | Communication budget | Strong families | Production rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Website/landing | Full narrative | Any, route-specific | Real text, semantic structure, exact facts, accessible forms |
| Social still | One image/headline/fact/action | Arena, Chrome-Blood, Rally, Road Case | Do not shrink a webpage into a tile |
| Vertical short video | Three beats | Shadow to Fire, Arena, Rally | Hook → reveal → fact/action; captions and safe flash behavior |
| Long video/trailer | Atmosphere + proof + payoff | Shadow, Love Letter, Arena | Cleared audio/video; readable fact hold |
| Search/display | Immediate relevance | One Night, Before First Chord | Minimal copy; message/landing match |
| Email | One primary purpose | Rally, Road Case, Before First Chord | Descriptive subject, plain facts, one primary CTA |
| Postcard/direct mail | Iconic front + factual back | One Night, Arena, Before First Chord | URL plus optional QR; print-safe contrast and expiry |
| Magazine/editorial | One monumental page | Arena, Love Letter, Chrome-Blood | Original poster language, not copied album art |
| Billboard/outdoor | Five-second read | Arena, One Night | Name + date/durable promise + location + short action |
| Press/booking | Evidence-led | Built to Land, Love Letter | Public-safe package; direct contact; no internal controls |
| Merchandise | Durable symbol | Chrome-Blood, Road Case | Item-specific rights, material, production, fulfillment checks |
| App/notification | Utility-first | Rally, Road Case, Before First Chord | Plain notifications; no false urgency |
| Game/interactive | Discovery/progression | Shadow, Road Case | Original motifs; do not gamify real safety controls |

## Core master formats

Plan masters before derivatives: vertical poster/feed master, square, 9:16, wide landscape, small display, event/header crop, and print only when required. Keep identity/headline/date/action in crop-safe zones. Critical facts remain live text wherever the medium allows.

## Paid, organic, owned, earned

- **Paid:** exact audience, spend/window owner, destination, tracking, policy review, and kill condition required.
- **Organic:** still needs rights/fact/accessibility review; “unpaid” is not “uncontrolled.”
- **Owned:** web/email can carry more facts and should serve as correction authority for short-form pieces.
- **Earned/partner:** supply a locked sharing kit with approved caption, image, link, short disclaimer, expiry, and contact for corrections.

## QR and links

Never rely on QR alone. Provide a readable short URL where feasible. QR/link destination must be tested, mobile usable, privacy appropriate, and stable for the placement lifetime. Preserve UTM parameters without exposing personal data.
