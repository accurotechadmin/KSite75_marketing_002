# Creative Review Scorecard

Score 0 (absent/wrong), 1 (partial), or 2 (strong). Target at least 24/30, with no zero in identity, facts, rights, safety/privacy, accessibility, action, or destination congruence.

| Criterion | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Is Just One KISS named and independently framed? |
| Family | Is one lead marketing family unmistakable? |
| Audience | Does the piece solve one defined audience problem? |
| Hierarchy | Is there one visual center and one message? |
| Brand world | Is black/chrome/fire recognizable without cliché? |
| Originality | Is expression original and non-official-looking? |
| Voice | Is copy bold, direct, and clear for its mode? |
| Proof | Does visible evidence support the promise? |
| Facts | Are every volatile fact and offer sourced/current? |
| Action | Is one next step obvious and honestly labeled? |
| Destination | Does the first destination view fulfill the action? |
| Rights | Are asset, mark, likeness, font, audio, and media rights resolved? |
| Safety/privacy | Are applicable advisories, consent, and data use plain? |
| Accessibility | Is the piece perceivable and adaptable? |
| Lifecycle | Are owner, release window, expiry, and rollback known? |

## Automatic holds

Unsupported affiliation; stale critical fact; unresolved asset or fan-media rights; misleading literal-effect implication; removed/misleading advisory; inaccessible critical information; unclear consent; broken/mismatched destination; prohibited platform content; missing final approver; or no expiry for a dated piece.
