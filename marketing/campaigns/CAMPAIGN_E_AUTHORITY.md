- **Audience lens:** `AUD-E` / Authority & Premium
- **Core question:** Is this a thoughtful, credible choice?

- **Voice:** Composed, precise, assured.

- **Destination:** `site/index.html?lens=E#proof`

## Funnel sequence

1. Awareness: restrained :15 brand-authority film.
2. Consideration: verified public-safe dossier or case story.
3. Interaction: request an overview or ask a qualified question.
4. Conversion: inquiry form with fit-setting and response expectations.
5. WOM: permissioned partner case-study conversation.

## Guardrails

Do not expose sensitive technical controls, imply certifications, promise safety, list capabilities, name partners, or publish performance outcomes without verification and permission.
