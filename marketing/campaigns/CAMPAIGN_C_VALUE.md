# Campaign C — Big Night. Clear Next Step.

- **Audience lens:** `AUD-C` / Value & Convenience
- **Core question:** Is this worth my attention, and is it easy to approach?
- **Current belief:** A theatrical experience may be confusing, inaccessible, or full of hidden friction.
- **Desired belief:** The spectacle can be monumental while the next step remains plain and low-risk.
- **Lead canon narrative:** One night becomes reachable through exact facts.
- **Proposition:** Big feeling. No maze. Get only the verified details you need.
- **Proof:** Current fact card, clear destination, transparent value exchange, tested mobile interaction.
- **Concept:** Oversized benefit headline split by a bright utility rail: KNOW / PLAN / GO or WATCH / LEARN / JOIN depending on the approved objective.
- **Visual grammar:** Punchy condensed type, diagonal heat bar, large numbered steps, high-contrast CTA, minimal atmosphere.
- **Voice:** Fast, literal, useful.
- **Primary CTA:** `GET THE SIGNAL`
- **Destination:** `site/index.html?lens=C#signal`
- **Primary event:** `start_signal_form`
- **Channel focus:** Meta feed/retargeting, search/display, email, programmatic display, mobile utility cards.

## Funnel sequence

1. Awareness: “Arena-scale imagination. Reachable connection.”
2. Consideration: what it is / what it is not / what to do next.
3. Interaction: save a current practical card.
4. Conversion: request verified updates with explicit consent.
5. WOM: share a locked fact card and canonical link.

## Guardrails

Do not use price, “free,” dates, countdowns, scarcity, guarantees, or logistics until they exist in an approved campaign overlay. Convenience claims must match the real destination and response process.
