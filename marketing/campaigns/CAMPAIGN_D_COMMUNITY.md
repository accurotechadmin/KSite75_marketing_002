# Campaign D — We Complete the Ritual

- **Audience lens:** `AUD-D` / Social & Community
- **Core question:** Do people like me belong here?
- **Current belief:** Tribute spectacle is performer-centered and the audience is incidental.
- **Desired belief:** Participation is welcomed, respected, and essential to the story.
- **Lead canon narrative:** The crowd completes the ritual; the road case stays open.
- **Proposition:** The stage starts the signal. The crowd makes it live.
- **Proof:** Cleared first-person stories, real questions, community artifacts, and documented permission.
- **Concept:** A creator-native contact sheet: handheld frames, voice-note waveform, comment-shaped modules, and one polished chrome signal cutting across them.
- **Visual grammar:** Warm grain, off-axis framing, modular snapshots, handwritten annotation, signal-red thread.
- **Voice:** Direct, plural, invitational, never manufactured.
- **Primary CTA:** `JOIN THE SIGNAL`
- **Destination:** `site/index.html?lens=D#community`
- **Primary event:** `start_signal_form`
- **Channel focus:** TikTok, Reels, Shorts, creator collaborations, community email, organic social.

## Funnel sequence

1. Awareness: creator-perspective “you are part of this” hook.
2. Consideration: permissioned story/reaction sequence.
3. Interaction: poll, Q&A, or low-risk signal signup.
4. Conversion: join updates with an exact value exchange.
5. WOM: contribution request with itemized channels, term, edit, paid, and revocation rights.

## Guardrails

No invented comments, composite testimonials presented as real, unlicensed fan media, coerced enthusiasm, or blanket reuse permissions. Minors and recognizable people require specific review.
