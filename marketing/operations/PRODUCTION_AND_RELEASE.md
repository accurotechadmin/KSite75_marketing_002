# Production and Release Workflow

## Gate 0 — Intake

Record requester, objective, audience, funnel job, lead family, channel, deadline, budget status, destination, known facts, and decision owner. Reject “make something cool” until the audience change and action are named.

## Gate 1 — Source lock

Create a fact sheet with source path/token/record, exact value, owner, verified timestamp, and expiry. Create an asset shortlist with provenance/status. Identify required disclaimer, advisory, consent, privacy, and accessibility treatments.

## Gate 2 — Brief approval

Approve strategy before execution: proposition, family, proof, copy hierarchy, composition, variants, CTA/destination, measurement, and constraints. Approval here authorizes production—not publication.

## Gate 3 — Concept review

Review low-cost concepts for family fit, one-center hierarchy, originality, crop behavior, and claim risk. Select one direction and record rejected directions/why.

## Gate 4 — Production

Build masters with controlled text layers, accessible alternatives, source links, file naming, and derivative map. Do not let export variants silently change facts or CTA meaning.

## Gate 5 — Specialist review

Run copy/fact, rights, safety/privacy, accessibility, channel/policy, destination/analytics, and technical export review. Record reviewer, decision, date, conditions, and evidence. “Looks good” is not a specialist review.

## Gate 6 — Release lock

Create an immutable release record listing exact copy, export paths/checksums, URLs/UTMs, audience, spend cap if applicable, schedule, approvers, monitoring owner, expiry, kill conditions, and rollback/replacement assets. Only this package may publish.

## Gate 7 — Publish and verify

After publication, verify rendering, crop, caption/alt text, link, analytics event, form behavior, date/time, targeting, spend, and placement context. Capture evidence or screenshots where appropriate.

## Gate 8 — Monitor

Watch delivery, action quality, comments/questions, broken links, fact changes, policy flags, accessibility/safety concerns, privacy incidents, and spend pacing. Pause first when harm or factual uncertainty appears.

## Gate 9 — Expire and learn

Remove dated work on time, stop paid delivery, redirect/retire destinations deliberately, archive exact releases and evidence, record metrics and qualitative learning, and identify whether the family/hypothesis—not just the image—worked.

## Change and incident handling

- **Fact change:** pause affected pieces, update authority first, issue corrected release, verify replacement.
- **Rights challenge:** unpublish/hold, preserve evidence, escalate to owner/reviewer, do not argue publicly from an unverified assumption.
- **Safety/access concern:** prioritize clear correction and direct assistance over brand voice.
- **Privacy issue:** stop collection/use as appropriate, preserve logs, route to responsible owner.
- **Broken destination:** pause ads and update links; do not keep buying unusable traffic.
