# Measurement and Experimentation

## Measurement principle

Measure whether the intended audience moved to the intended next state—not whether a dramatic image accumulated vanity engagement. Pair quantitative signals with quality, rights, safety, accessibility, and community observations.

## Stage measures

| Job | Primary indicators | Diagnostic indicators | Guardrails |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Awareness | Qualified reach, destination visits, aided/direct recognition proxies | Hold/view rate, frequency, geographic delivery | Negative confusion, official-affiliation misunderstanding |
| Interest | Spectacle/about views, meaningful video consumption | Saves, carousel depth, engaged time | Effect misunderstanding, accessibility complaints |
| Consideration | Directions/FAQ actions, practical-page engagement | Scroll depth, contact questions | Repeated unresolved logistics, stale facts |
| Conversion | Valid RSVP/update completion, direction action | Form start/completion, CTA rate | Consent quality, duplicates, delivery failures |
| Retention | Return visits, vault engagement, valid contributions/shares | Email engagement, content depth | Unclear permissions, moderation burden |
| Booking | Qualified inquiries | Overview downloads, contact completion | Poor-fit volume, capability misunderstanding |

## Experiment rules

- Change one meaningful variable: family, headline structure, proof image, CTA, audience, or placement—not all at once.
- Hold facts, disclaimer meaning, safety treatment, consent, destination function, and basic accessibility constant.
- State hypothesis, primary metric, guardrails, sample/window expectation, stop conditions, and decision rule before launch.
- Do not optimize toward clicks with misleading urgency, cropped warnings, false official cues, or inaccessible design.
- Record inconclusive results; absence of proof is not proof of no effect.

## Suggested events and UTM discipline

Use documented names such as `view_campaign_landing`, `click_event_details`, `click_directions`, `start_lead_form`, `complete_lead_form`, `open_vault_item`, `submit_contact`, and `download_booking_overview`. Standardize `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, and `utm_content`; never place personal or sensitive information in URLs.

## Learning agenda for the nine families

Test which family creates qualified attention by audience, which proof object improves understanding, whether utility-led creative increases attendance planning, what value statement improves consented signup, what vault content earns return visits, and which professional proof improves inquiry fit. Do not rank families on a single universal leaderboard; they perform different jobs.

## Reporting cadence

- **Launch check:** rendering, links, delivery, major errors.
- **Active review:** pacing, quality, anomalies, audience questions.
- **Decision review:** continue, revise, reallocate, or stop against prewritten rule.
- **Postmortem:** hypothesis, result, guardrails, creative/family learning, operational learning, reusable evidence, next test.
