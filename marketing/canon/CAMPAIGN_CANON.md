# Campaign-Facing Canon

**Portable authority:** `BRAND_CANON.md` and `BRAND_CANON.json` contain the controlling standalone truth. This document is its creative quick reference.

## Brand promise

Just One KISS presents an independent theatrical rock tribute as a reachable, “you had to be there” event: black stage depth, chrome craft, fire-colored energy, smoke and hard light, a monumental central presence, disciplined production intent, and a crowd invited to complete the ritual.

## Creative formula

> **Black first. Chrome second. Fire third. Mythic scale always. Ordinary never.**

This is a hierarchy, not a requirement to cover every surface with effects. Black creates anticipation and rest. Chrome makes the world physical and crafted. Fire concentrates attention and action. Mythic scale enlarges the emotional promise. Utility information remains unusually clear.

## Productive tensions

| Hold both | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Arena scale + reachable local event | Extraordinary but attainable |
| Theatrical menace + fan welcome | Thrilling, not hostile |
| Dangerous-looking image + controlled intent | Excitement with trust |
| Mythic copy + literal utility | Imagination with confidence |
| Fan recognition + original expression | Cultural fluency without false official status |
| Visual excess + simple hierarchy | Memorable and usable |
| Singular performer + crowd participation | Icon plus community |
| One-night urgency + continuing vault | Action now and relationship later |

## Visual constants

- Near-black dominates; bone white carries primary legibility.
- Chrome supplies structure, borders, armor, hardware, and secondary actions.
- Red/orange supplies heat and primary action; yellow supplies factual certainty and focus.
- One central subject: performer, silhouette, portal, road case, rig, object, or fact stack.
- Display type creates a peak; utility type handles facts, forms, consent, warnings, and directions.
- Preferred material language: leather, rivets, road-case edges, armor plates, truss, haze, reflective floor.
- Preferred devices: portal, silhouette, poster stack, hot/cold CTA pair, yellow fact stamp, numbered proof card, orange rule, road case, call-and-answer, disciplined warning.

## Voice constants

Bold, direct, fan-literate, and sensory; never falsely official, needlessly hostile, or vague where facts matter. Use **impact → image → fact → action**. Headlines can be fragments. Warnings, consent, directions, and disclaimers use ordinary complete language.

## Durable story arcs

1. The arena arrives.
2. Shadow turns into fire.
3. One night becomes reachable through exact facts.
4. Fan devotion becomes original craft—a love letter with volume.
5. The road case opens and the audience carries the story forward.

## Minimum viable signal

For a very small placement: black field, one original chrome/fire/performer motif, correct `Just One KISS` identity, one promise or verified fact, one action/destination, and required rights/disclaimer treatment.

## Non-negotiable public controls

- Name the independent tribute accurately and never imply official affiliation, sponsorship, authorization, or endorsement.
- Use only original or cleared imagery, type, music, footage, marks, likenesses, and fan media.
- Verify volatile date, venue, admission, camping, parking, contact, access, offer, and destination facts at release.
- Do not imply literal fire, explosions, or pyrotechnics from metaphorical “fire” language.
- Keep loud sound, bright light, fog, and flashing-pattern advisories visible where placement and conversion context require them.
- Preserve semantic text, contrast, focus, alt text, captions, reduced-motion treatment, readable hold times, and non-color cues.
- State collection purpose and consent clearly; do not overpromise response times or reuse rights.

## Drift test

Ask: Could this advertise any local rock event? Could it look official? Is the one action obvious? Are the facts current? Does it balance myth and clarity? A wrong answer triggers revision or hold.
