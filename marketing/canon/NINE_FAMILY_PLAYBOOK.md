## Selection method

1. Write the audience's current belief and the next belief needed.
2. Select the family whose emotional movement bridges them.
3. Select one proof object that actually exists.
4. Select one action that the destination can fulfill.
5. Add the minimum verified facts for the placement.
6. Apply rights, safety, consent, and accessibility controls.
7. Remove every visual or line that does not serve the bridge.

## Combination rule

A lead family owns headline, opening image, and emotional movement. A supporting family may supply proof or the practical close. If two families compete for the headline, create a sequence rather than a collage.
