# Just One KISS Portable Brand Canon

**Document ID:** `jok.brand-canon.portable`  
**Version:** `1.0.0`  
**Status:** controlled internal canon snapshot  
**Scope:** evergreen; contains no event date, price, venue, or availability claim

This is the controlling brand source inside the portable `marketing/` package. Campaign work must not depend on files outside this folder. Change this document and `BRAND_CANON.json` together, then review every downstream expression record.

## Brand truth

**Just One KISS is an independent theatrical rock tribute that turns disciplined original craft into a reachable, arena-scale communal experience.**

It is a love letter with volume: fan recognition expressed through original performance, production intent, and a crowd invited to complete the ritual. It must never be framed as officially affiliated, sponsored, authorized, or endorsed by the artists being celebrated.

## Promise

An extraordinary theatrical-rock encounter can feel monumental without feeling remote: crafted with intent, communicated honestly, and opened to the people who make the night complete.

## Positioning

- **Category:** independent theatrical rock tribute.
- **Difference:** arena-scale imagination joined to reachable participation and disciplined production craft.
- **Audience value:** spectacle, recognition, clarity, and belonging.
- **Independence:** cultural fluency without borrowed authority.

## Personality and worldview

Bold, direct, fan-literate, sensory, disciplined, welcoming, original, and practical when facts matter. The world is built from anticipation, transformation, proof, participation, and a story that continues after the first encounter.

## Master narrative

1. **The arena arrives:** something larger than ordinary life becomes reachable.
2. **Shadow turns to fire:** anticipation becomes revelation through controlled theatrical craft.
3. **The craft becomes visible:** original performance and preparation earn belief.
4. **The crowd completes the ritual:** participation turns spectacle into belonging.
5. **The road case stays open:** artifacts, stories, and connection carry the experience forward.

## Visual DNA

> **Black first. Chrome second. Fire third. Mythic scale always. Ordinary never.**

- Near-black establishes stage depth and rest.
- Bone white carries primary legibility.
- Chrome creates structure, craft, armor, and reflective proof.
- Red/orange concentrates heat, revelation, and primary action.
- Signal yellow identifies verified facts and utility.
- One visual center dominates; supporting information obeys a strict hierarchy.
- Display typography creates one peak; utility typography stays literal and highly readable.
- Materials include road cases, rivets, truss, leather, haze, hard light, reflective floors, and original stage hardware.

## Voice

Use **impact → image → fact → action**. Headlines may be fragments. Proof must be observable. Utility, warnings, consent, directions, and disclaimers use ordinary complete language.

## Approved evergreen claim set

- `identity`: “Just One KISS”
- `category`: “An independent theatrical rock tribute.”
- `story`: “A love letter with volume.”
- `experience`: “Arena-scale imagination. Reachable connection.”
- `community`: “The crowd completes the ritual.”
- `continuity`: “The road case stays open.”

These are creative statements, not proof of any particular venue capability, effect, schedule, affiliation, or future event.

## Non-negotiable controls

1. Never imply official affiliation, sponsorship, authorization, or endorsement.
2. Use only original or cleared imagery, marks, likenesses, fonts, music, footage, and community media.
3. Treat fire as color and energy unless a literal effect is separately verified and approved.
4. Never invent dates, admission, venue, access, capabilities, popularity, reviews, or scarcity.
5. Keep applicable sensory information calm, visible, and literal.
6. Provide contrast, semantic text, focus, alt text, captions, reduced motion, readable hold times, and non-color cues.
7. Explain collection purpose and consent; do not imply blanket reuse permission.
8. One audience problem, one proposition, one proof, and one primary action per piece.

## Canon change protocol

A proposed change requires an owner, rationale, version increment, review date, and impact review of `expression/`, `campaigns/`, `site/`, assets, and release records. Campaign performance may inform a canon review but may not silently rewrite brand truth.
