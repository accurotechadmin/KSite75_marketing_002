# SSOT Brand Canon → Multi-Audience Marketing Campaign System

## Purpose

This document translates a stable SSOT (Single Source of Truth) brand canon into a reusable marketing campaign architecture.

The governing principle is simple:

> **The SSOT brand story stays fixed; the audience translation, proof type, visual grammar, CTA, and media placement change.**

This prevents five campaigns from becoming five inconsistent brands.

The brand remains invariant across all campaigns:
- Brand promise
- Positioning
- Personality
- Worldview
- Differentiators
- Proof
- Visual DNA
- Narrative arc

What changes by campaign:
- Audience reason to care
- Evidence foregrounded
- Visual grammar
- CTA
- Media environment
- Funnel-stage objective

---

# 1. Five Campaign Lenses

Rather than defining the five audiences only by age, gender, or demographic categories, define them primarily by motivation and buying psychology. These audience types can work across many industries.

| Campaign | Generic audience | Their core question | Visual marketing approach | Story emphasis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. The Aspirational** | Identity-, lifestyle-, possibility-driven | “Who could I become with this?” | **Cinematic / editorial** — premium photography, atmosphere, whitespace, dramatic typography, emotionally led video | Vision, transformation, identity, possibility |
| **B. The Rational Evaluator** | Researcher, skeptic, comparison shopper | “Why should I believe you?” | **Evidence / information design** — diagrams, comparison cards, demonstrations, numbers, annotated imagery | Mechanism, differentiators, proof, credibility |
| **C. The Value & Convenience Buyer** | Wants an obvious practical win | “Is this worth it, and is it easy?” | **High-energy direct response** — big benefit copy, offer architecture, punchy design, demonstrations, before/after | Benefit, friction reduction, price/value, immediacy |
| **D. The Social / Community Buyer** | Trusts people more than institutions | “Do people like me actually like this?” | **Creator-native / UGC / documentary** — handheld, real environments, testimonials, reactions, screenshots, comments | People, belonging, social proof, lived experience |
| **E. The Authority / Premium Buyer** | Risk-sensitive, quality/status/professional orientation | “Is this the best, safest, or smartest choice?” | **Institutional / premium authority** — controlled photography, expert voices, restrained motion, polished data, case studies | Expertise, quality, provenance, reputation, risk reduction |

These are **five doors into the same house**. Campaign A should not claim anything Campaign B would contradict. Each campaign simply provides a different reason to believe the same SSOT proposition.

# 2. The Five-Stage Funnel

The campaign system becomes a **25-cell matrix: 5 audiences × 5 funnel stages**.

| Funnel stage | Job of the message | Best creative | Primary CTA | Best media environments |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Brand Awareness** | Make the audience recognize, remember, and emotionally categorize the brand | Hero films, vertical short video, striking stills, creator introductions, audio | Watch / Discover / Learn | Meta, YouTube, TikTok, CTV, Spotify/podcasts, Pinterest, LinkedIn, Reddit, Snap, creators |
| **2. Consideration** | Explain why the brand is different and why the audience should believe it | Explainers, demonstrations, carousels, comparisons, testimonials, case studies | Learn More / See How / Compare | YouTube, Search, Meta, LinkedIn, Reddit, Pinterest, TikTok, website/content |
| **3. Interaction** | Gain a low-friction behavioral commitment | Quiz, calculator, configurator, sample, guide, webinar, email signup, lead form, save/share | Try / Get / Calculate / Download / Ask | Landing pages, Search, Meta Lead Ads, LinkedIn Lead Gen, TikTok, Pinterest, email capture |
| **4. Conversion & Sale** | Resolve objections and give the audience a reason to act now | Offer ads, product ads, testimonial + CTA, retargeting, comparison, guarantee, sales page | Buy / Book / Start / Request Quote | Search, Shopping, Meta, remarketing, email/SMS, LinkedIn for B2B, retailer/marketplace media |
| **5. Post-Conversion / WOM** | Turn customers into retention, evidence, and acquisition | Onboarding, how-to, cross-sell, review request, referral, UGC ask, customer stories | Review / Refer / Share / Upgrade | Email, SMS, CRM audiences, customer communities, social, review platforms, referral pages |

The funnel should feed itself:

> **Stage 5 testimonials, reviews, referrals, and customer stories become Stage 1–3 proof for the next audience cohort.**

# 3. Canonical Media Masters

Do **not** create a completely different file from scratch for every platform.

Build a reusable media library around a small number of production masters.

## Core Raster / Video Artboards

| Master | Size | Primary use |
|---|---:|---|
| **Vertical full screen** | **1080 × 1920 — 9:16** | Reels, Stories, TikTok, Shorts, Snapchat, vertical video |
| **Portrait feed** | **1080 × 1350 — 4:5** | Meta feed, LinkedIn, Reddit, mobile feed placements |
| **Square** | **1080 × 1080 or 1200 × 1200 — 1:1** | Carousels, feeds, companion artwork, general display use |
| **Landscape social/display** | **1200 × 628 — 1.91:1** | Google, LinkedIn, display, social placements |
| **HD landscape video** | **1920 × 1080 — 16:9** | YouTube, CTV, LinkedIn video, website video |
| **Pinterest portrait** | **1000 × 1500 — 2:3** | Pinterest primary format |
| **Audio master** | **:30 plus :15 cut** | Spotify, podcasts, streaming audio |
| **CTV cuts** | **1920 × 1080; :06 / :15 / :30** | Streaming TV, programmatic video |

### Production Principle

For hero video shoots, compose footage so it survives:
- 9:16
- 4:5
- 16:9

Do not shoot 16:9 first and assume a vertical ad can always be cropped from it successfully.

# 5. Campaign × Funnel Creative Matrix

Each campaign should tell the same brand story through a different cognitive and visual route.

| Campaign | Awareness | Consideration | Interaction | Conversion | Post-Sale / WOM |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Cinematic aspiration** | :15 hero film | Lifestyle story | “Find your…” quiz | Aspirational offer | “Show us yours” share |
| **B — Evidence / proof** | Surprising claim or statistic | Explainer / carousel | Calculator / assessment | Comparison + guarantee | Results / review request |
| **C — Direct value** | Big benefit / problem | Demo / before-after | Coupon / sample / configurator | Offer + urgency | Reorder / cross-sell / referral incentive |
| **D — Creator / community** | Creator hook | Testimonial / UGC sequence | Poll / comment / Q&A | Creator endorsement + CTA | UGC / review / referral ask |
| **E — Premium authority** | Brand authority film | Expert / case study | Guide / webinar / consultation | Consultation / demo / premium purchase | VIP nurture / case-study ask |

# 6. Recommended Creative Production Matrix

For **each major creative concept**, build a reusable asset family.

| Asset family | Recommended versions |
|---|---|
| **Hero video** | :30, :15, :06 |
| **Vertical social video** | 9:16 × :30 / :15 / :06 |
| **Horizontal video** | 16:9 × :30 / :15 / :06 |
| **Feed video** | 4:5 × :15 / :06 |
| **Hero still** | 4:5, 1:1, 1.91:1, 9:16 |
| **Carousel** | 5–8 cards in 1:1; optional 2:3 where platform-specific |
| **Copy** | 5 hooks × 3 headlines × 3 body lengths × 3 CTAs |
| **Proof** | Testimonial, statistic, demonstration, comparison, authority credential |
| **Landing page** | Campaign-lens hero + SSOT-controlled body / proof / offer |
| **Email** | Consideration, abandonment, conversion, onboarding, review, referral |
| **Audio** | :30 + :15 |
| **Thumbnail / cover** | 16:9, 1:1, 9:16 |
| **Creator kit** | Brief + talking points + prohibited claims + raw-footage requirements |

The operating principle is:

> **Story component → canonical format → placement adaptation**

Do not think first in terms of “Instagram asset,” “TikTok asset,” or “LinkedIn asset.”

Think first in terms of:
1. Brand story
2. Audience lens
3. Funnel job
4. Creative concept
5. Canonical asset
6. Placement adaptation

# 7. Marketing Expression Layer for the SSOT

The brand canon should gain a dedicated **Marketing Expression Layer** underneath it.

Recommended hierarchy:

> **Brand Truth → Narrative → Audience Lens → Funnel Stage → Message Proposition → Proof → Creative Concept → Asset → Placement → CTA → Landing Destination → Measurement Event**

This hierarchy provides:
- Traceability from every ad back to the SSOT
- Consistent storytelling
- Controlled variation
- Easier creative review
- Easier asset reuse
- Easier automation
- Easier measurement
- Easier governance

# 8. Asset Naming Convention

Give every piece of media a structured identifier.

Example:

```text
BRAND / AUD-D / STAGE-02 / CONCEPT-03 / VIDEO / 9x16 / 15S / V04
```

A practical normalized filename could be:

```text
BRAND_AUD-D_S02_C03_VIDEO_9x16_15S_V04.mp4
```

Suggested components:
- Brand
- Audience
- Funnel stage
- Creative concept
- Asset type
- Aspect ratio
- Duration
- Language / region, if needed
- Offer, if needed
- Version
- Date or campaign wave, if needed

This turns the marketing library into a system instead of a folder full of files such as:

```text
final_FINAL_v7_revised2.mp4
```

# 10. Recommended Strategic Principle

The goal is not to advertise on every available channel.

The goal is to create a media architecture in which:

- The **brand canon** defines what can never change.
- The **audience lens** determines what matters most.
- The **funnel stage** determines the job of the message.
- The **creative approach** determines how the story is expressed.
- The **placement** determines how the asset must be formatted.
- The **measurement event** determines whether the asset succeeded.

A mature system therefore looks like this:

```text
                         SSOT BRAND CANON
                                │
                         MASTER NARRATIVE
                                │
        ┌───────────────┬───────┼───────┬───────────────┐
        │               │       │       │               │
   ASPIRATION         PROOF    VALUE  COMMUNITY      AUTHORITY
        │               │       │       │               │
        └───────────────┴───────┼───────┴───────────────┘
                                │
                         5-STAGE FUNNEL
                                │
        Awareness → Consideration → Interaction → Conversion
                                │
                       Post-Sale / WOM
                                │
                         CREATIVE SYSTEM
                                │
                 CANONICAL FORMAT LIBRARY
                                │
                  PLATFORM-SPECIFIC ADAPTATION
                                │
                     MEASUREMENT + LEARNING
                                │
                         BACK INTO SSOT
```

# 11. Final Operating Model

The complete campaign system can be thought of as a controlled combinatorial model:

```text
5 audience lenses
×
5 funnel stages
×
multiple creative concepts
×
canonical media formats
×
selected placements
×
copy / offer / CTA variants
```

The SSOT prevents this system from fragmenting.

The campaign architecture provides enough variation to:
- Reach different buying psychologies
- Test different messages
- Produce distinctive creative
- Match different media environments
- Preserve one coherent brand
- Learn which story routes generate the strongest response

The core takeaway:

> **Do not build five brands. Build one brand with five audience translations, expressed through five distinct visual systems, moving through one common funnel architecture.**
