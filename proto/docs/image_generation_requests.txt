GLOBAL IMAGE-GENERATION RULES FOR EVERY IMAGE
- Output file format: .webp.
- No text baked into the image. The website supplies all text.
- No official KISS logos, album art, outside photos, exact restricted makeup designs, outside costumes, official fonts, or restricted media.
- Keep the public vibe: black first, chrome second, fire third; mythic scale always; ordinary never.
- Make images usable as responsive section backgrounds: strong subject, but leave safe dark negative space for big white page text.
- Prefer cinematic, high-contrast, dark-stage imagery with red/orange/gold fire light, chrome highlights, fog/haze, and road-case/pavilion/show-control textures.
- Avoid gore, weapons, political symbols, platform logos, readable private data, or anything that implies outside partnership.
- Recommended master aspect ratio: 16:9 landscape at 2400x1350 unless noted. Also keep the subject readable when cropped center/left/right by CSS.
