# Public Release Gate Policy

Gate families are rights, safety, privacy, venue, accessibility, copy, media, technical disclosure, and brand affiliation. Gate states are blocked, needs review, conditional, approved internal, approved public, and not applicable.

Public blocker rule: no public copy may imply official endorsement, sponsorship, authorization, ownership, or clearance by KISS, Gene Simmons, Pophouse, or related rightsholders unless written approval exists.

Safety-sensitive effects such as fog, strobes, bright lights, projection, loud sound, blackout states, moving lights, drops, and platforms remain venue-aware and review-gated.
