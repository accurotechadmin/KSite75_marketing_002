# September 26 Marketing Session Intake

Use this after the boot sequence. The session should ask the user for the smallest set of answers needed to begin, not force completion of the entire form when the task is exploratory.

## Essential request

1. What should be created—strategy, theme, copy, image, layout, video, audio, landing page, ad set, export package, or review?
2. Is this **current-theme fidelity**, **new-theme exploration**, or a **controlled hybrid**?
3. Who is it for and what should they believe/do next?
4. Which channels, placements, sizes, durations, locale, and deadline are in scope?
5. What is the primary CTA and destination?
6. Which specific files/images/reference materials must be used or avoided?
7. Is this a concept, internal production master, or publication-ready release?

## Fact-lock questions when relevant

- Confirm full year, timezone, and whether 7:30 PM means doors or show start.
- Confirm venue, city/address, in-person/virtual status.
- Confirm admission/price/fees/capacity/registration rules.
- Confirm practical, access, age, weather/cancellation, and sensory facts.
- Confirm performer billing, sponsors/partners, public contact, and approved destination.

## Creative-direction questions when relevant

- Which current elements must stay recognizable?
- For a new theme: desired feeling, references to learn from (not copy), exclusions, palette/type preferences.
- Required hero/proof; available photography/footage/audio; whether new assets may be generated.
- Text density, language/locale, accessibility needs, and approval constraints.
- Variants or experiment hypothesis.

## Production questions when relevant

- Editable source format and tool constraints.
- Delivery naming/path, color profile, transparency, file-size/codec limits.
- Paid vs organic use, geography/term, and rights evidence.
- Budget, schedule/timezone, campaign/account ownership, platform eligibility.
- Tracking stack, conversion definition, UTMs, consent, and reporting owner.

## Default behavior

If the user asks for concept exploration, proceed with conspicuous placeholders and a blocked-facts list. If the user asks for publication-ready material, do not proceed past the relevant gate with unknown critical facts or rights. Offer the highest-fidelity safe intermediate deliverable instead.
