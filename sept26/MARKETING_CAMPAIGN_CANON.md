## 4. Evergreen brand canon

### 4.1 Brand truth

Just One KISS is an independent theatrical rock tribute that turns disciplined original craft into a reachable, arena-scale communal experience. It is a love letter with volume: fan recognition expressed through original performance, production intent, and a crowd invited to complete the ritual.

### 4.2 Promise and positioning

- **Promise:** monumental theatrical-rock energy can feel reachable, crafted, honest, and communal.
- **Difference:** arena-scale imagination paired with disciplined production craft and practical audience clarity.
- **Audience value:** spectacle, recognition, anticipation, belonging, and confidence about the next step.
- **Independence:** cultural fluency without borrowed authority.

### 4.3 Master narrative

1. **The arena arrives:** something beyond ordinary life becomes reachable.
2. **Shadow turns to fire:** anticipation becomes revelation.
3. **The craft becomes visible:** preparation and original performance earn belief.
4. **The crowd completes the ritual:** participation turns spectacle into belonging.
5. **The road case stays open:** artifacts and stories continue the relationship.

### 4.4 Personality

Bold, direct, fan-literate, sensory, disciplined, welcoming, original, and literal when facts matter. Theatrical is not the same as aggressive; practical clarity is part of hospitality.

## 5. Visual identity and art direction

### 5.1 Governing formula

> **Black first. Chrome second. Fire third. Mythic scale always. Ordinary never.**

This is a hierarchy, not a mandate to add effects everywhere.

- Near-black creates stage depth and visual rest.
- Bone white carries essential legibility.
- Chrome communicates structure, hardware, armor, and craft.
- Red/orange concentrates heat, transformation, and primary action.
- Signal yellow identifies verified facts, warnings, and utility.
- One center dominates; facts and CTA follow a strict hierarchy.

### 5.2 Implemented palette reference

### 5.3 Material and image vocabulary

Preferred: original stage portal, silhouette, chrome/leather/rivet macro, lighting rig, controlled haze, reflective floor, backstage control dossier, tactical/arrival map, road case, candid community portrait, performer/attraction proof.

Avoid: generic local-band flyers, stock “rock hands,” fake sold-out crowds, official-looking logos, copied album artwork, exact protected makeup/designs, gore, uncontrolled flame claims, unreadable generated text, or AI artifacts presented as documentary proof.

### 5.4 Composition rules

- One visual center and one primary action per piece.
- Keep date/time/venue/offer/CTA as editable live typography.
- Generate or photograph atmosphere without critical small lettering.
- Capture with 9:16, 4:5, and 16:9 adaptations in mind.
- Use a central crop-safe nucleus, but intentionally recompose rather than blind crop.
- Reserve UI-safe top/bottom/edge zones in vertical creative; confirm them in placement previews.
- Small banners get identity, one fact, and one action—not a miniature poster.

### 5.5 Existing candidate asset families

### 5.6 Campaign theme modes

Every brief declares **current-theme fidelity**, **new-theme exploration**, or a **controlled hybrid**. Current-theme work extends the system and emotional logic rather than copying screenshot pixels. A new theme is a governed campaign expression—not permission to change brand identity, confirmed facts, independence, rights, accessibility, safety, consent, or release controls. ## 6. Voice, copy, and claims

### 6.1 Formula

Use **impact → image → fact → action**.

- **Headline:** usually 4–9 words; bold, memorable, one idea.
- **Support:** one observable image, benefit, or proof.
- **Fact block:** only current verified facts required for the placement.
- **CTA:** one honest verb whose destination immediately fulfills it.
- **Controls:** plain-language disclaimer/advisory/terms as needed.

### 6.2 Tone modes

| Mode | May be theatrical? | Must remain literal? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero/awareness | Yes | Identity and event facts |
| Spectacle/interest | Yes | Actual effects and capabilities |
| Community | Warm and fan-literate | Value exchange and permissions |
| Utility | Minimal | Date, time, venue, price, directions, access |
| Consent/privacy | No | Collection, use, retention, opt-out |
| Safety | No | Advisory meaning |
| Disclaimer | No | Independence meaning |
| Press/buyer | Moderately | Proof, capabilities, availability, contact |

### 6.3 Approved evergreen seed language

These are draftable building blocks, not automatic release approval:

- Just One KISS.
- An independent theatrical rock tribute.
- A love letter with volume.
- Arena-scale imagination. Reachable connection.
- The crowd completes the ritual.
- The road case stays open.
- Enter the larger night.
- The craft behind the heat.
- Built with intent.
- Before the first chord.

### 6.4 September 26 copy framework

Until fact lock, use placeholders only for unresolved fields in design files. Every creative must already carry the confirmed date, time, address, and phone:

Official, authorized, endorsed, biggest, best, sold out, selling fast, last tickets, free, no ticket required, family-friendly, safe for everyone, accessible, pyrotechnics, exact attendance, invented testimonials, partner/sponsor names, guaranteed response times, price/value comparisons, or urgency unsupported by a real deadline/capacity.

## 9. Campaign objectives and KPI framework

### 9.1 Primary objective

**Decision required.** Choose exactly one primary business outcome:

- completed paid ticket purchase;
- completed free registration/RSVP;
- consented update-list completion;
- qualified landing-page/event-details view; or
- another owner-approved measurable action.

Do not optimize to clicks when the real outcome is completion or attendance.

### 9.2 Metric definitions

| Metric | Definition/use |
| --- | --- |
| Reach | Unique accounts/people exposed, platform-estimated |
| Frequency | Impressions ÷ reach; monitor fatigue by audience/window |
| CPM | Spend ÷ impressions × 1,000 |
| CTR | Clicks ÷ impressions; state link vs all-click CTR |
| CPC | Spend ÷ clicks; use matching click definition |
| Landing-page views | Loaded/qualified destination views, not raw clicks |
| Registration starts | First meaningful interaction with registration |
| Completed registrations | Server/platform-confirmed completion; deduplicate |
| Completion rate | Completed registrations ÷ starts or qualified LPVs; label denominator |
| CPA | Spend ÷ deduplicated completed registrations |
| Revenue | Valid completed order revenue net/gross definition documented |
| ROAS | Attributed revenue ÷ ad spend; only if revenue/attribution is valid |
| Creative-ratio performance | Metrics split by actual served ratio/placement |
| Platform/audience performance | Comparable windows and attribution definitions required |

No target numbers are canon until budget, capacity, conversion, baseline, and attribution are known.

### 14.2 UTM standard

`utm_source=<platform>`

`utm_term=<keyword-or-targeting-cell>` only when useful and non-sensitive.

Use lowercase ASCII snake case. Never put names, emails, phone numbers, health/access needs, or other personal/sensitive data in URLs.

### 14.3 Naming convention

Asset: `JOK_SEP26_<YEAR>_AUD-<A-F>_S<01-05>_C<01-99>_<TYPE>_<RATIO>_<DURATION>_<LOCALE>_V<NN>.<ext>`

### 14.4 Privacy and QA

- Document tag/pixel lawful basis and consent behavior before activation.

## 17. Accessibility, safety, rights, and legal controls

### Accessibility

- Critical facts exist as text, not only inside imagery/audio.
- Check contrast in every crop; muted gray over imagery requires measurement.
- Provide concise alt text or mark decorative media appropriately.
- Caption all spoken/sung informational content; retain transcript files.
- Ensure meaningful on-screen copy has readable size and hold time.
- Avoid rapid flashing; review applicable thresholds and create reduced-motion/static alternatives.
- Never convey offer/status only through red/green/color.
- Landing pages support keyboard, focus, 200%/400% zoom, reflow, labels, errors, notices, and no-JS essentials.

### Rights record minimum

Asset ID, creator/source, creation date, original/derivative status, identifiable people, releases, trademarks/protected designs, font/audio licenses, paid/organic usage, edits allowed, channels, territory, term, credit, revocation, restrictions, checksum, approver, approval date, expiry.

### Tribute posture

Never imply official affiliation, sponsorship, authorization, endorsement, or ownership by the celebrated artists. Avoid official logos, album art, recordings, photos, exact restricted makeup, or other protected expression without documented rights. “Inspired by” does not itself create permission.

### Testimonials and social proof

Require authentic exact language, speaker identity/relationship, date/context, substantiation, disclosure of material connection, written permission, edit approval, channel/paid-use scope, term, and a non-misleading presentation. No verified testimonial was found for this campaign.

### Safety

Only advertise literal capabilities/effects confirmed for the September 26 production. If loud sound, bright light, fog, flashing, or strobe-style patterns apply, place calm plain advisories in consideration/conversion contexts and at the destination. Do not turn warnings into decorative spectacle.
