## Decisions and rationale

- Used the ten channels explicitly enumerated in `docs/ssot/settings/marketing_channels.json` and their ten repository prompt packs as the bounded catalogue scope.
- Retained every enumerated platform placement/dimension, producing 50 one-shot prompts rather than collapsing same-ratio placements into generic prompts.
- Used Earl's phone as the only action cue because the primary CTA and destination are unresolved. This does not establish a reusable CTA decision for later releases.
- Treated the prototype set as visual learning only: mythic hierarchy, title impact, spectacle density and performer dominance may inform original work, while copied marks, exact designs, unsafe claims, clutter and stale facts are prohibited.
- Required each downstream session to verify volatile platform specifications against a current primary source.

These are implementation choices within the owner-requested draft library, not claims of public approval. No separate durable owner-decision record was required.

The prompt library requires exact typesetting, readable mandatory facts, UI-safe placement, contrast/non-color checks, placement-specific alt text and scorecard review.
