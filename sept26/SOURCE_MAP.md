## Platform research policy

Platform requirements change. The dimensions in the production checklist are owner-requested production targets, not a guarantee of placement eligibility. At channel setup and again immediately before export/spend, archive the current official requirement and record the verification date.

Official starting points:

- Meta Ads Guide: <https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide>
- TikTok ad specifications: <https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/video-ads-specifications>
- Google image ad sizes: <https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722096>
- Google video requirements: <https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/13547298>
- Google Demand Gen: <https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/17091672>
- LinkedIn ad specifications: <https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a427650>
- Pinterest product specifications: <https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/pinterest-product-specs>
- Snapchat ad formats: <https://forbusiness.snapchat.com/advertising/ad-formats>
- X creative specifications: <https://business.x.com/en/help/campaign-setup/creative-ad-specifications>
- Reddit ad unit specifications: <https://business.reddithelp.com/s/article/Reddit-Ad-Unit-Specifications>

Record aspect ratio, pixel range, duration, file type/weight, text/caption rules, safe zones, audio policy, CTA availability, destination policy, bid/budget minimum, tracking prerequisites, and commercial eligibility. Ads Manager is the final operational check for account- and market-specific availability.
