## 7. Guardrails and verification

- Substantiate benefits, performance claims, comparisons, statistics, testimonials, and demonstrations.
- Label simulations, prototypes, dramatizations, composites, or AI-generated elements when required.
- Do not imply that a depicted result is typical unless verified.
- Use approved customer/person likenesses and releases.
- Avoid unapproved competitor marks, copied trade dress, official-looking affiliation, or protected creative references.
- Include applicable safety, age, accessibility, privacy, and material-terms language.
- Reconfirm platform dimensions and advertising policies before export.
