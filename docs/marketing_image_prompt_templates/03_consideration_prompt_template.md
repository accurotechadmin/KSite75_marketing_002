Use with `00_shared_template_contract.md`.

## 1. Campaign role

Give an interested audience enough verified practical information to decide whether `[project, product, service, event, offer, program, custom]` fits their needs.

- Campaign stage: `[consideration, planning, comparison, qualification, decision support, custom]`
- Audience moment: `[interested but needs details, comparing options, planning attendance, checking eligibility, evaluating cost, custom]`
- Campaign job: `[reduce uncertainty, explain logistics, organize facts, answer common questions, show options, custom]`
- Desired audience response: `[plan, compare, check eligibility, get directions, review details, contact support, custom]`

## 4. Art direction

Create a practical information design that remains visually connected to the campaign while prioritizing clarity.

- Visual style: `[editorial information card, modular dashboard, numbered guide, map-inspired layout, comparison grid, clean infographic, photographic utility card, custom]`
- Information density: `[low, medium, high but readable, custom]`
- Layout: `[five-card stack, three-column summary, two-column comparison, numbered vertical guide, map plus facts, custom]`
- Background: `[quiet brand texture, clean neutral field, subtle environmental image, dark high-contrast panel, custom]`
- Accent system: `[number badges, icons, dividers, chips, route lines, checkmarks only when verified, custom]`
- Type treatment: `[clear sans serif, editorial hierarchy, condensed labels with readable body copy, brand type system, custom]`

Use less decorative image density than an awareness or interest asset. Facts and decision support should remain understandable on a phone.

## 5. Composition notes

- Put the decision headline and highest-priority fact in the upper safe zone.
- Organize `[3, 4, 5, custom]` practical facts into clearly separated cards or columns.
- Use icons only when their meaning is unambiguous and culturally appropriate.
- If using a map, route, address, schedule, price table, or comparison, verify every displayed detail.
- Distinguish confirmed information from `[estimate, tentative, subject to change, contact for details, custom]` using reviewed wording.
- Place applicable safety, eligibility, accessibility, or material-terms notes next to the related action or fact rather than hiding them in decorative microtext.

## 6. Platform adaptations

| Platform / placement | Planning size | Adaptation |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Meta / Instagram square | 1440x1440 / 1:1 | Use three to five compact fact cards; place project/date/category chips across the top; preserve generous spacing. |
| Vertical feed | 1440x1800 / 4:5 | Use a large headline followed by stacked or staggered fact cards; place CTA and supporting note in the lower safe zone. |
| Stories/Reels still | 1080x1920 or 1440x2560 / 9:16 | Use `[one complete vertical guide, two coordinated story cards, custom]`; protect top/bottom UI areas and keep each card focused. |
| Google / X / LinkedIn landscape | 1200x628 or 1440x754 / 1.91:1 | Simplify to three columns or one headline plus three priority facts; move secondary detail to the destination page. |
| Google square | 1200x1200 / 1:1 | Use one decision headline, three key facts, and one CTA. |
| Google portrait | 960x1200 / 4:5 | Recompose fact cards vertically; avoid shrinking a landscape comparison table. |
| Small display | 300x250 | Use one practical headline, one or two essential facts, one brand/project identifier, and one CTA. |
| Local print flyer | 8.5x11 in or 4x6 in | Add full verified details, reviewed QR, safe margins, bleed, and readable accessibility/safety/material-terms notes. |
| Header/event crop | 1920x1005 or 1500x500 | Use one headline and up to three short facts; place detailed logistics on the linked page. |

## 7. Guardrails and verification

- Reverify dates, hours, time zones, addresses, maps, prices, taxes/fees, availability, capacity, parking, shipping, eligibility, age rules, accessibility, weather, security, refund/cancellation terms, and contact information as applicable.
- Do not state that access, parking, inventory, delivery, accommodations, safety, or availability is guaranteed unless it is genuinely guaranteed and approved.
- Avoid presenting estimates as confirmed facts.
- Ensure comparisons use equivalent criteria and current information.
- Do not hide material limitations in illegible text.
- Use approved maps, icons, photos, logos, and product imagery.
- Reconfirm platform specifications before export.
