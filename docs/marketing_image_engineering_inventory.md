## Canonical standard: what every marketing piece should feel like

> **The active /proto/public site: black first, chrome second, fire third, mythic scale always; practical information remains exceptionally clear.**

### Construction rules

- near-black stage void provides negative space
- symmetrical or strongly anchored portal/object silhouette
- hard chrome specular edges and industrial framing
- red/orange fire light supplies urgency; bone-white and yellow support facts
- fog/haze makes beams dimensional without obscuring essential content
- oversized condensed display hierarchy paired with plain readable utility text
- Lead with one dominant idea, one focal silhouette and one next action.
- Separate expressive imagery from live HTML/text facts wherever possible.
- Recompose for each ratio; never blind-crop central figures, hands, instruments, flames or factual panels.
- Preserve a clear dark copy zone and test overlays at mobile and desktop breakpoints.
- Do not imply official affiliation; do not publish exact protected marks, album art, official logos or uncleared likenesses.
- Operational authenticity—truss, road cases, cables, controls, armor construction—must look intentional, controlled and plausible.

### Palette roles

- **Black:** dominant field, depth and anticipation
- **Chrome:** structure, proof, precision and premium craft
- **Fire Red Orange:** energy, urgency and CTA emphasis
- **Bone White:** primary type and hard highlights
- **Signal Yellow:** eyebrows, fact chips and wayfinding
- **Green:** minor live-light counterpoint only, never the core brand color

### Drift blockers

- bright generic corporate layouts
- flat clip-art flames
- crowded collages without hierarchy
- unreadable type baked into generated images
- unverified map directions or event facts
- casual workshop/reference backgrounds in finished campaigns
- AI artifacts in faces, hands, instruments, hardware, typography or cables

## Status model

- **Runtime benchmark:** named `/proto/public/assets/img` assets and the captured `/proto/public` screenshots define the current experiential target, while still requiring normal rights/release review.
- **Concept:** poster and social masters are exploratory and must not be mistaken for approved event facts.
- **Reference only:** performer/source/third-party/canon imagery establishes identity, venue, materials or provenance; it is not automatically publishable.
- **Opaque uploads:** timestamped layer uploads require provenance and slot approval before reuse.
