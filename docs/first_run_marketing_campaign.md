# Just One KISS - First-Run Still Image Marketing Campaign

**Purpose:** Define the first Just One KISS still-image marketing campaign as a five-piece customer-journey sequence. This is a planning and production-description document only; it does **not** create final images.

**Status:** Campaign creative brief / still-image asset plan.

**Timeline classification:** `GEN` / GENERAL / NOT TIMELINE-SPECIFIC. These campaign assets promote the event ecosystem as a whole rather than one precise show cue.

**Primary sources:** current public-site screenshot supplied by the owner, `docs/brand_story_style_guide_inventory.md`, `docs/marketing_still_image_inventory.md`, `docs/styleguide.md`, `docs/knowledge.md`, `README.md`, `proto/docs/language.json`, `proto/docs/website_ssot.md`, and `docs/ssot/ads_specs.md`.

**Image-production scope:** Create no actual images yet. Each entry below describes the artwork, copy lockup, platform adaptations, safety/rights posture, and export family needed for later design or image-generation work.

---

## 1. Campaign Thesis

The first campaign should behave like a month-by-month fan journey, not a random poster dump. Each still image should feel like it belongs to the same theatrical world: black-dominant, chrome-framed, fire-lit, huge white display type, yellow fact chips, red/orange calls to action, smoke/fog atmosphere, road-case texture, stage truss silhouettes, and practical facts kept readable.

The funnel is adapted from a sales model into a free-show attendance model:

| Funnel stage | Just One KISS audience action | Campaign job | Primary CTA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Awareness | Learn that the show exists. | Announce the night and create instant recognition. | Remember July 25. |
| Interest | Understand why it is worth attention. | Sell the spectacle, mood, and fan ritual. | See what the night feels like. |
| Consideration | Decide whether it is practical to attend. | Explain free admission, location, camping, safety, and arrival basics. | Plan the trip / get directions. |
| Conversion | Commit without paid ticket language. | Drive RSVP or update-list signup. | Get the July 25 drop. |
| Retention & Nurture | Share, invite, ask questions, and return for updates. | Turn attendees into promoters and community participants. | Tell your crew / open the road case. |

Because the show is free, the campaign must not use “buy,” “tickets on sale,” “purchase,” “checkout,” VIP, paid admission, or scarcity language that implies a paid transaction. “RSVP appreciated,” “Join updates,” “Tell your crew,” “Plan your night,” and “No ticket required” are the conversion language.

---

## 2. Campaign-Wide Creative Rules

1. **One visual world:** Every asset should feel like a crop from the current site: black void, red smoke, chrome edges, gold/yellow badges, massive block headlines, and theatrical contrast.
2. **Readable facts:** Event name, date, free admission, venue, location, and key safety/disclaimer lines must remain legible as real text in production files.
3. **Rights-aware tribute posture:** Do not use official logos, exact protected makeup, album art, official photos, or official-sounding affiliation language. Use original theatrical tribute imagery: silhouette, chrome armor hints, stage lights, fog, road cases, and fire-colored atmosphere.
4. **Safety-aware conversion:** Near any RSVP, attendance, directions, or plan-your-night CTA, include compact language noting loud sound, bright lights, fog, and flashing/strobe-style looks.
5. **Modular layout:** Each artwork concept should have a master composition and crop-safe variants for square, vertical feed, story/reel, landscape, display, header, and print formats.
6. **No final image generation yet:** Treat each “art direction” block as a future design prompt or creative brief, not as generated art.

### 3.2 Safe-zone behavior

- Keep the main headline and date in the central 70% of square and vertical crops.
- For 9:16 story assets, keep headline, date, and CTA away from top/bottom UI overlays.
- For 1.91:1 landscape, move practical fact chips into a lower horizontal strip and keep the central image simple.
- For 300x250 banners, use only: `Just One KISS`, `July 25`, `Free show`, `Interlochen / US 31`, and a short CTA.

## 4. The Five-Piece Funnel Campaign

## Stage 1 — Awareness

### Campaign piece 01: **“The Night Exists”**

**Audience moment:** A cold audience first learns that Just One KISS is happening.

**Core message:** `FREE SHOW · JULY 25, 2026 · CYCLE MOORE LEGACY · INTERLOCHEN / US 31`

**Primary headline options:**

1. `YOU WANTED THE BEST?`
2. `ONE NIGHT. FULL FIRE.`
3. `JULY 25 IS COMING.`

**Recommended final headline lockup:** `YOU WANTED THE BEST?` above `JULY 25 IS COMING.`

**Subhead:** `A free theatrical rock tribute night at Cycle Moore Legacy in Interlochen.`

**CTA:** `Save July 25` / `Join updates`

**Art direction:** A black arena-void poster with a giant white compressed headline slamming forward. Behind it: original chrome shards or stage-truss geometry, red/orange smoke rolling along the bottom, and a distant stage-light flare. The performer is suggested only as an original rim-lit silhouette or shadow form, not as exact protected makeup or official imagery. Use yellow fact chips like the current site: `Free show`, `No ticket required`, `RSVP appreciated`.

**Composition notes:**

- Top: small yellow eyebrow line with the verified event facts.
- Center: huge stacked white headline, slightly distressed or beveled.
- Right/back: chrome-fire visual mass for depth.
- Bottom: compact disclaimer strip: `Independent theatrical rock tribute. No outside partnership claimed or implied.`

**Platform adaptations:**

| Platform / placement | Asset description |
| --- | --- |
| Meta feed square / 1:1 | Center the headline; keep chips at lower third; CTA button-style pill at bottom. |
| Meta and Instagram 4:5 feed | Let the headline dominate the top two-thirds; smoke/venue facts in bottom card. |
| Stories / Reels 9:16 still | Hero headline at mid-frame; date in a large yellow vertical badge; CTA at lower safe zone. |
| X / LinkedIn / Google 1.91:1 | Use a wide truss-and-smoke banner with the date and free-show facts in a strong left text block. |
| Google 300x250 | Simplified lockup: `Just One KISS / July 25 / Free show / Interlochen`. |
| Print flyer | Add QR placeholder for update-list page, larger venue line, and compact safety/disclaimer footer. |

**Review notes:** `GEN`; public-facing; rights review required before final art; safety/disclaimer footer required for print and landing-page-driving variants.

## Stage 2 — Interest

### Campaign piece 02: **“The Ritual”**

**Audience moment:** The audience has seen the announcement and now needs to feel why this is different from a generic cover night.

**Core message:** `A STAGE TAKEOVER, NOT A POLITE COVER NIGHT.`

**Primary headline options:**

1. `THE RITUAL STARTS HERE.`
2. `NOT A POLITE COVER NIGHT.`
3. `FIRE-LIT. CHROME-BLOODED. LOUD.`

**Recommended final headline lockup:** `THE RITUAL STARTS HERE.` with `Not a polite cover night.` as the secondary line.

**Subhead:** `A fan-facing night of loud music, bright lights, fog, fire-colored atmosphere, and theatrical tribute energy.`

**CTA:** `See the spectacle` / `Get the July 25 drop`

**Art direction:** A dramatic section-card style image inspired by the current “About the Show” area. Use a dark stage floor, low red fog, chrome border panels, light beams, and four numbered proof tiles: `01 Demon-scale entrance`, `02 Fire-lit singalong`, `03 One controlled blast`, `04 Chrome-blood spectacle`. These are mood promises, not technical cue disclosures.

**Composition notes:**

- Create a four-card grid inside a larger chrome/red frame.
- Use short sensory phrases only; avoid exposing cue timing or internal show-control details.
- The image should feel like someone opened a backstage road case and found a mythic event card inside.

**Platform adaptations:**

| Platform / placement | Asset description |
| --- | --- |
| Instagram carousel | Use this as either one composite 1:1 card or four individual proof cards with consistent numbering. |
| Meta feed 4:5 | Stack headline on top, then two-by-two proof grid below. |
| Stories 9:16 | Turn proof cards into a vertical numbered ladder; CTA on bottom. |
| Google responsive display | Use the strongest single claim: `A stage takeover, not a polite cover night.` |
| LinkedIn / venue-buyer feed | Slightly more polished version emphasizing “theatrical tribute event” and “free public event” without over-hyping danger. |
| X / Bluesky organic | Use the four-card grid as a shareable “what it feels like” image. |

**Review notes:** `GEN`; public copy must remain original; do not show official marks; keep safety phrasing visible when using “loud,” “fog,” or “flashing” language.

## Stage 3 — Consideration

### Campaign piece 03: **“Before You Roll In”**

**Audience moment:** The audience is interested but needs practical confidence: where, when, whether it costs money, whether camping exists, and what the environment is like.

**Core message:** `BEFORE YOU ROLL IN...`

**Primary headline options:**

1. `BEFORE YOU ROLL IN...`
2. `PLAN THE NIGHT.`
3. `FREE SHOW. REAL DETAILS.`

**Recommended final headline lockup:** `BEFORE YOU ROLL IN...`

**Subhead:** `Free admission. No ticket required. Cycle Moore Legacy. Interlochen on US 31.`

**CTA:** `Get directions` / `Plan the night`

**Art direction:** A practical info card that still feels theatrical. Use the current site’s “Before You Roll In...” panel language: black background, yellow numbered badges, red smoke glow, thin chrome/gold dividers, and five vertical info cards. Include: `Admission`, `Location / Parking`, `Camping`, `Fog / Strobes`, `BYOB and be safe`.

**Composition notes:**

- This is the clearest utility piece in the campaign.
- Use less fantasy image density so facts stay readable.
- Include address in larger variants: `11075 US 31 South, Interlochen, MI 49643`.
- Include camping distinction: `$10/night; regular Cycle Moore charges apply for longer stays.`

**Platform adaptations:**

| Platform / placement | Asset description |
| --- | --- |
| Meta / Instagram square | Five compact numbered cards; venue/date chips across top. |
| Vertical feed 4:5 | Use big headline top, then five stacked or staggered cards. |
| Stories 9:16 | Split into two story stills if needed: facts 1-3, then safety/BYOB. |
| Google / X / LinkedIn 1.91:1 | Use a simplified three-column layout: Free show, Location, Camping + safety. |
| Local print flyer | Add QR placeholder and full address; keep all five info cards. |
| Small display banner | Use only: `Free show · July 25 · Cycle Moore Legacy · Get directions`. |

**Review notes:** `GEN`; facts must match public SSOT before export; avoid overpromising parking, accessibility, weather, security, or camping operations beyond verified language.

## Stage 4 — Conversion Without Purchase

### Campaign piece 04: **“Get the July 25 Drop”**

**Audience moment:** A warm audience is ready to commit, but commitment means RSVP/update-list signup, not purchase.

**Core message:** `GET THE JULY 25 DROP.`

**Primary headline options:**

1. `GET THE JULY 25 DROP.`
2. `NO TICKET REQUIRED.`
3. `JOIN THE RALLY.`

**Recommended final headline lockup:** `GET THE JULY 25 DROP.` plus `Free show · RSVP appreciated` as a fact chip.

**Subhead:** `No ticket required. Updates, reminders, practical details, and fan vault drops go through the list.`

**CTA:** `Join updates` / `RSVP appreciated`

**Art direction:** A conversion-card design based on the current signup section: left side huge white headline over black/red stage texture; right side a mock form panel with email and city/zip fields as visual placeholders, a gold consent checkbox row, and a red-orange CTA button. This image should describe the action without pretending it is an actual form unless used as a website banner.

**Composition notes:**

- Keep `No spam` / `Safety and access notes are folded into the main event, arrival, media, and Fan Vault drops` as small supporting text where space allows.
- Keep CTA language friendly and practical; do not use “buy now” or “tickets.”
- Add compact safety line near lower edge on larger exports.

**Platform adaptations:**

| Platform / placement | Asset description |
| --- | --- |
| Meta / Instagram feed | Use strong split layout: headline left, form-card visual right. |
| Stories 9:16 | Stack headline, form-card mock, CTA, safety note vertically. |
| Google Display / Discovery | Use `Get the July 25 drop` with a clean CTA; avoid tiny mock form details in small crops. |
| X / Bluesky | Use square card with a large CTA button graphic and free-show chip. |
| LinkedIn | Use more utility-forward copy: `Get event updates for the July 25 free show.` |
| Print / QR card | Replace mock form with QR placeholder and `Join updates / RSVP appreciated`. |

**Review notes:** `GEN`; form/consent language must come from public-language SSOT before final image; include privacy/contact caveats in destination page rather than crowding small ads.

## Stage 5 — Retention & Nurture

### Campaign piece 05: **“Open the Road Case”**

**Audience moment:** The audience is signed up or close to attending; now invite sharing, questions, fan media, and repeat engagement.

**Core message:** `OPEN THE ROAD CASE.`

**Primary headline options:**

1. `OPEN THE ROAD CASE.`
2. `TELL YOUR CREW.`
3. `THE FAN VAULT IS OPEN.`

**Recommended final headline lockup:** `OPEN THE ROAD CASE.` with `Tell your crew. Bring the night with you.` as secondary line.

**Subhead:** `Show tips, fan media, Q&A, crowd questions, arrival notes, and July 25 updates live in the vault.`

**CTA:** `Tell your crew` / `Open the Fan Vault` / `Ask a question`

**Art direction:** A fan-vault / road-case image matching the current site’s card-grid section. Picture an original black touring road case opened toward the viewer, glowing red/yellow from inside, with chrome latches and six labeled compartments: `Free show`, `Video posts`, `Fan media`, `Tips`, `Q&A`, `Chat`. Use smoky depth, not literal official memorabilia.

**Composition notes:**

- This is the community/share piece, not the logistics piece.
- Include “Free show” and date in a small persistent chip so shares still carry basic context.
- Encourage sharing without implying contests, giveaways, or official fan-club status.

**Platform adaptations:**

| Platform / placement | Asset description |
| --- | --- |
| Instagram / Facebook carousel | Turn each vault compartment into a separate card if desired. |
| Square feed | Use open road case centered with six compartments and large headline above. |
| Stories 9:16 | Make it feel like a vertical road-case drawer; CTA sticker area at bottom. |
| X / Bluesky | Strong shareable square with `Tell your crew` and date chip. |
| Google remarketing | Simplified retargeting card: `Still coming July 25? Join updates.` |
| Local/community print | Small handout variant: `Free show · July 25 · tell your crew · join updates` with QR placeholder. |

**Review notes:** `GEN`; no contest/giveaway language; fan-media invitations must remain subject to the public site’s media/privacy/review posture.

## 5. Month-to-Month Rollout Sequence

| Timing | Stage | Recommended emphasis | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Month 1 | Awareness | Launch the night: date, free show, place. | Use piece 01 broadly across paid/organic/local. |
| Month 2 | Interest | Sell the ritual and stage takeover. | Use piece 02, especially carousel/story variants. |
| Month 3 | Consideration | Practical planning: location, camping, safety. | Use piece 03 and refresh as facts are reverified. |
| Month 4 | Conversion | RSVP/update-list push. | Use piece 04 with destination-page alignment. |
| Month 5 / final lead-up | Retention & Nurture | Share, ask, invite, return for updates. | Use piece 05 plus final reminder crops from pieces 03/04. |

If the timeline compresses, run pieces 01-02 for cold audiences, pieces 03-04 for warm/retargeting audiences, and piece 05 for people who engaged or signed up.
