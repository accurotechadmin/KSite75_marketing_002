# Facebook Ad Campaign Production Briefs - Five-Stage Image System

Source set: `docs/marketing_image_mockup_specs/`, beginning with `HOW_TO_USE.md` and using the shared campaign data plus all five stage specs.

## Shared Facebook production rules

- Campaign: Just One KISS, July 25, 2026, Cycle Moore Legacy, Interlochen, Michigan / US 31.
- Offer posture: free show; no ticket required; RSVP/update-list appreciated.
- Visual world: cinematic editorial event advertising with stage black, bone white, chrome silver, blood red, fire orange, KISS gold, electric yellow, black tailored fabric, chrome hardware, road-case texture, controlled smoke, gloss-black cards, gold accents, oversized condensed white display type, and readable utility text.
- Creative premise: the wild theatrical beast has been tamed into a polished, suited, professional event-campaign presence while remaining theatrical and dangerous in silhouette.
- Required guardrails: do not use official KISS logos, official album art, exact protected makeup, copied official costumes, restricted fonts, celebrity likeness replication, paid-ticket language, VIP language, scarcity claims, platform logos, or any official sponsorship/authorization implication. Do not invent show time, parking, traffic, weather, security, accessibility, capacity, or safety assurances.
- Fact/safety note: loud sound, bright lights, fog, and flashing/strobe-style looks may be used; include this where attendance or RSVP decisions are being prompted.
- Facebook-first crops: produce square feed at 1440x1440, vertical feed at 1440x1800, and Stories/Reels still at 1440x2560 unless a ticket below specifies otherwise. Recompose for each crop; do not center-crop the master.
- CTA destinations: use the current public event landing page and its RSVP/update-list, directions, FAQ/contact, or updates routes. Verify the live URL before export.
