# Batch D - Conversion Mockup Spec: Join the Guest List, Not a Checkout

Use with `00_shared_mockup_data.md`.

## 1. Campaign role

Encourage an informed audience to take one verified next action for Just One KISS without implying a paid ticket transaction.

- Campaign stage: conversion / free RSVP and update-list lead capture.
- Audience moment: ready to act after seeing announcement, spectacle, or practical details.
- Campaign job: make the next step clear, reduce friction, summarize material facts, and direct traffic.
- Desired audience action: join updates, RSVP, get directions, or ask a question.
- Conversion type: free registration / lead capture / event update signup.

## 2. Filled campaign variables

- Project/brand: Just One KISS.
- Offer/action: join the July 25 update list or RSVP for reminders.
- Value received: event updates, practical details, reminders, and route/directions information when available.
- Price/admission: free show; no ticket required.
- Availability or deadline: July 25, 2026 event date; no scarcity claim.
- Material terms: destination page contains current details, privacy/consent language, and any final attendance notes.
- Form fields represented visually: email only, unless the final form verifies additional fields.
- Consent/privacy context: concise privacy/consent summary to match the final form; production placeholder is `Use the event form for updates. Review privacy details on the destination page.`
- Classification: `GEN`.
- CTA destination: the RSVP/update-list route as the primary conversion target, with directions and contact routes reserved for utility variants.

## 3. Final selected copy

- Eyebrow: `READY WHEN YOU ARE`
- Primary headline: `JOIN THE JULY 25 LIST.`
- Secondary line: `Free show. No ticket required.`
- CTA: `Join updates`
- Reassurance line: `No payment required.`
- Supporting note: `Loud sound, bright lights, fog, and flashing/strobe-style looks may be used.`

## 4. Art direction

Create a focused action composition styled like a premium black invitation card on a chrome clipboard or road-case lid. The visual should feel calm, clear, and confident: one dominant CTA, one email-field illustration, one date badge, one free-show note, and restrained red-gold glow. Do not make the static ad appear deceptively interactive.

## 5. Composition notes

- Use one primary CTA only: `Join updates`.
- Keep the free-show/no-ticket fact within the first visual scan.
- Place safety note near the action area.
- For small placements, remove mock field details and retain only brand, date, free-show fact, and CTA.
- If using a QR code in print, generate and test the final code outside the image-generation process.
- Use no approved screenshots unless the current real form state is verified.

## 7. Guardrails and verification

- Verify final form fields, privacy language, destination URL, QR code, and tracking parameters before launch.
- Do not fabricate urgency, scarcity, capacity, endorsement, reviews, savings, guarantees, or ticketing.
- Do not preselect consent in a depicted form unless legally appropriate and representative of the real experience.
- Avoid deceptive buttons, notification badges, fake system alerts, or fake interface states.
- Include safety and independent-tribute language where space allows.
- Reconfirm platform dimensions and ad rules before launch.

## 8. Output request

Create three conversion compositions: a square update-list card, a 9:16 story action card, and a 4x6 QR print card. Use one dominant CTA and display only verified offer, value, material facts, and terms above. Deliver editable text zones, finished-typesetting guidance, text-free alternate, alt-text draft, and filename pattern.

## 9. Alt-text draft

A polished black, chrome, and red-gold Just One KISS update-list card inviting viewers to join updates for the free July 25, 2026 show with a no-payment-required note and safety reminder.
