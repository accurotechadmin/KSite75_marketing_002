# Batch C - Consideration Mockup Spec: Before You Arrive

Use with `00_shared_mockup_data.md`.

## 1. Campaign role

Give an interested audience enough verified practical information to decide whether attending Just One KISS fits their plans.

- Campaign stage: consideration / planning support.
- Audience moment: interested but needs practical details before committing or sharing.
- Campaign job: reduce uncertainty, explain logistics, organize facts, answer common questions, and keep safety information visible.
- Desired audience response: plan the visit, get directions, review details, ask questions, and join updates.

## 2. Filled campaign variables

- Project/brand: Just One KISS.
- Decision question: What should I know before I arrive?
- Practical facts: July 25, 2026; free show; no ticket required; Cycle Moore Legacy; 11075 US 31 South, Interlochen, MI 49643; camping `$10/night` with regular Cycle Moore charges for longer stays; loud sound, bright lights, fog, and flashing/strobe-style looks may be used.
- Options or tiers: not applicable.
- Eligibility or requirements: omit until verified.
- Accessibility information: use the event contact/FAQ route for questions; do not state specific access details beyond verified sources.
- Safety/environment information: loud sound, bright lights, fog, and flashing/strobe-style looks may be used.
- Classification: `GEN`.
- CTA destination: the event details page, with directions as the preferred click target for practical-planning placements and FAQ/contact as the secondary support path.

## 3. Final selected copy

- Eyebrow: `BEFORE YOU ARRIVE`
- Primary headline: `PLAN THE NIGHT. KEEP THE SUIT SHARP.`
- Secondary line: `Free show. No ticket required. Interlochen / US 31.`
- Fact-card labels: `Date`; `Admission`; `Location`; `Camping`; `Safety`
- CTA: `Plan your night`
- Supporting note: `Details should be checked on the event page before travel.`

## 4. Art direction

Create a practical information design that feels like a premium black event itinerary tucked inside a chrome road case. Use a quiet dark texture, gold numbered badges, clean white type, red-orange dividers, and subtle smoke. Reduce fantasy density; practical facts must be easy to read on a phone.

## 5. Composition notes

- Put `BEFORE YOU ARRIVE` and the primary planning headline in the upper safe zone.
- Organize five practical facts into clearly separated cards.
- Use icons only if unambiguous: calendar, open door/free, location pin, tent, light/fog warning.
- Verify every displayed address and camping statement before production export.
- Put the safety note next to the planning CTA, not hidden in microtext.
- Mark unknown accessibility, parking, traffic, show time, and weather details as omitted rather than guessed.

## 7. Guardrails and verification

- Reverify date, address, camping statement, CTA URL, and any map/directions details before export.
- Do not state parking, accessibility, weather, security, capacity, show time, or traffic information unless verified.
- Do not guarantee access, accommodations, safety, availability, or venue operations.
- Keep safety and camping distinctions readable.
- Use approved maps, icons, photos, or original graphic elements only.
- Reconfirm platform specifications before export.

## 8. Output request

Create three consideration-stage compositions: a square five-fact card, a 9:16 two-card story sequence, and a local print flyer layout. Prioritize clarity and professional confidence over spectacle density. Deliver editable text zones, QR placeholder guidance, alt-text draft, and filename pattern.

## 9. Alt-text draft

A polished black-and-gold Just One KISS planning card with clear event facts for July 25, 2026, free admission, Cycle Moore Legacy in Interlochen, camping information, and a safety notice.
