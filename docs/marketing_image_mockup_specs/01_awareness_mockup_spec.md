# Batch A - Awareness Mockup Spec: The Polite Monster Arrives

Use with `00_shared_mockup_data.md`.

## 1. Campaign role

Introduce Just One KISS to a cold local/regional and classic-rock audience as a polished free theatrical rock tribute event.

- Campaign stage: awareness / launch announcement.
- Audience moment: first exposure and date announcement.
- Campaign job: make the name memorable, establish the refined beast-in-a-suit visual system, announce verified facts, and create instant recognition.
- Desired audience response: remember July 25, recognize the visual system, save the date, and visit the event page.

## 2. Filled campaign variables

- Project/brand: Just One KISS.
- Featured subject: original suited theatrical tribute silhouette with chrome hardware, polite smile implied through posture rather than celebrity likeness, red-gold rim light, black stage void, and controlled smoke.
- Core announcement: Just One KISS is a free theatrical rock tribute event on July 25, 2026 at Cycle Moore Legacy in Interlochen, Michigan.
- Highest-priority fact: July 25, 2026.
- Secondary facts: Free show; Cycle Moore Legacy; Interlochen / US 31; no ticket required; RSVP/update-list appreciated.
- Classification: `GEN`.
- CTA destination: the current Just One KISS event landing page, with the update-list route as the preferred click target for paid/organic awareness ads.

## 3. Final selected copy

- Eyebrow: `A FREE THEATRICAL ROCK TRIBUTE EVENT`
- Primary headline: `THE MONSTER HAS A RESERVATION.`
- Secondary headline: `JULY 25, 2026`
- Subhead: `Just One KISS brings a polished black-chrome-fire tribute night to Cycle Moore Legacy.`
- Fact chips: `Free show`; `No ticket required`; `Interlochen / US 31`
- CTA: `Save July 25`
- Supporting note: `Independent theatrical rock tribute. No outside partnership claimed or implied.`

## 4. Art direction

Create a cinematic editorial event-ad composition: black tailored suit, chrome lapel pin or cuff hardware, red-orange smoke glow behind the shoulders, precise white headline typography, and gold/yellow fact chips. The subject should feel like the wild beast has learned table manners without losing its fangs. Use an original silhouette or symbolic suited figure only; do not depict exact protected makeup, official costume details, official logos, or celebrity likeness replication.

## 5. Composition notes

- Place the eyebrow in the upper safe zone as a small gold label.
- Let `THE MONSTER HAS A RESERVATION.` dominate the center in oversized white condensed type.
- Place the suited silhouette on the right or behind the type as a controlled editorial hero presence.
- Place July 25 as a gold date seal or chrome-framed badge.
- Put fact chips in the lower third.
- Put the CTA as a red-orange pill near the bottom safe zone.
- Use the relationship disclaimer as a compact footer when space allows.

## 7. Guardrails and verification

- Verify final CTA URL before export.
- Do not imply official KISS or Gene Simmons authorization.
- Do not use paid-ticket language.
- Do not add show time, parking, weather, capacity, or accessibility claims.
- Keep event name, date, free-show status, and location as readable real text.
- Reconfirm platform dimensions and current ad policies before paid spend.

## 8. Output request

Create three original awareness compositions for the first mockup set: square social, vertical feed, and landscape/header. Use the polished suit campaign system, central date memory, and verified facts above. Deliver editable text-zone guidance, text-free background alternate direction, alt-text draft, filename pattern, and export checklist.

## 9. Alt-text draft

A polished black-and-chrome event poster for Just One KISS showing a shadowed suited theatrical figure in red-gold smoke with text announcing a free July 25, 2026 show at Cycle Moore Legacy in Interlochen.
