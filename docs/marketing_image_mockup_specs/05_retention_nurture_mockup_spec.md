# Batch E - Retention and Nurture Mockup Spec: The Road Case Opens

Use with `00_shared_mockup_data.md`.

## 1. Campaign role

Encourage warm audiences, update-list subscribers, followers, and interested attendees to return, share, ask questions, and stay connected before the event.

- Campaign stage: retention / nurture / community.
- Audience moment: signed up, followed, engaged, asked a question, or visited the event page.
- Campaign job: remind, help, invite sharing, answer questions, and create a polished return path.
- Desired audience response: return for details, share with a friend, ask a question, get directions, or bring a crew.

## 2. Filled campaign variables

- Project/brand: Just One KISS.
- Audience relationship: update-list subscriber, warm follower, interested attendee, or community participant.
- Next-value promise: useful event updates, practical planning reminders, fan-facing share prompts, and FAQ/contact routes.
- Nurture modules: `Bring the crew`; `Plan the ride`; `Check safety notes`; `Open the fan vault`; `Ask a question`; `Save the date`.
- Sharing invitation: tell your crew.
- Support/contact route: the current event FAQ/contact route.
- Incentive: none.
- Classification: `GEN`.
- CTA destination: the updates page as the primary nurture path, with FAQ/contact, directions, fan-vault/community, and event page routes used for module-specific variants.

## 3. Final selected copy

- Eyebrow: `WHAT'S NEXT`
- Primary headline: `OPEN THE ROAD CASE.`
- Secondary line: `The details are getting sharper.`
- Module labels: `Tell your crew`; `Plan the ride`; `Check safety`; `Ask a question`; `Save July 25`; `Join updates`
- CTA: `Tell your crew`
- Supporting note: `Use approved media only when sharing. No outside partnership claimed or implied.`

## 4. Art direction

Create an inviting return-path visual: a polished black road case opened like a luxury briefcase, with chrome latches, red-gold interior glow, tidy compartments, and clean labeled cards. It should feel like the beast's professional travel kit: organized, gleaming, theatrical, and ready. No official memorabilia, logos, album art, protected makeup, or copied costume pieces.

## 5. Composition notes

- Use six modules or return paths.
- Keep Just One KISS and July 25 visible so reshared assets remain understandable.
- Separate share actions from practical planning and support actions.
- Use user-generated content only with permission and review; the first mockup should use abstract placeholder cards instead.
- Do not show progress, rewards, points, community size, or membership levels.
- Include communication preference or unsubscribe context only in email-specific variants where required.
- Avoid manipulative guilt, false loss, fake activity notifications, or manufactured social proof.

## 7. Guardrails and verification

- Verify actual subscriber, email, sharing, and contact workflows before personalizing.
- Do not expose private information or infer sensitive traits.
- Use consented communication channels and required preference/unsubscribe controls for email variants.
- Do not fabricate testimonials, community size, activity, popularity, reviews, countdowns, scarcity, incentives, or referral rewards.
- Obtain permission for any user-generated content, names, handles, photos, or quotes.
- Apply media/privacy, accessibility, safety, legal, and platform-policy reviews.
- Reconfirm platform dimensions and destination URLs before publication.

## 8. Output request

Create three retention/nurture compositions: a square road-case module card, a carousel cover-plus-module sequence, and an email/header crop. Present six return reasons and one primary CTA. Deliver editable text zones, text-free alternate, alt-text draft, and filename pattern.

## 9. Alt-text draft

A polished black road case opened like a luxury briefcase with chrome latches and red-gold glow, showing labeled Just One KISS update cards for sharing, planning, safety notes, questions, saving July 25, and joining updates.
