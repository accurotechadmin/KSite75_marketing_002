# Batch B - Interest Mockup Spec: The Suit Is Not the Show

Use with `00_shared_mockup_data.md`.

## 1. Campaign role

Help an audience that recognizes Just One KISS understand why the event deserves attention beyond the announcement.

- Campaign stage: interest / differentiation.
- Audience moment: has seen the announcement and is curious but not yet emotionally committed.
- Campaign job: demonstrate the refined spectacle, communicate the theatrical value, show differentiators, and build desire without exposing internal cue mechanics.
- Desired audience response: explore the event page, imagine attending, share the mood, and join updates.

## 2. Filled campaign variables

- Project/brand: Just One KISS.
- Core value proposition: a free, polished, theatrical rock tribute night with arena-scale visual language translated into a professional public event experience.
- Differentiators: tailored black-chrome-fire visual identity; fan-facing tribute energy; practical free-show clarity; theatrical lights, fog, and stage atmosphere; professional presentation for attendees and venue buyers.
- Experience or outcome: a controlled high-impact night that feels mythic, loud, polished, and worth planning around without guaranteeing individual outcomes.
- Proof type: sensory experience and feature proof cards.
- Featured subject: editorial proof-grid system using suit details, chrome case hardware, red fog, stage lights, and abstract performer silhouette.
- Classification: `GEN`.

## 3. Final selected copy

- Eyebrow: `INSIDE THE EXPERIENCE`
- Primary headline: `TAILORED. THEATRICAL. LOUD.`
- Secondary line: `A stage-sized tribute night dressed for the room.`
- Subhead: `Black suit discipline meets chrome, fire, fog, and fan-facing rock spectacle.`
- Proof labels: `Polished invitation`; `Chrome-stage atmosphere`; `Free-show clarity`; `Fan-night energy`
- CTA: `See the spectacle`
- Supporting note: `Loud sound, bright lights, fog, and flashing/strobe-style looks may be used.`

## 4. Art direction

Build a refined proof system using a four-card editorial grid inside a chrome-black frame. Each card should show one original visual metaphor: a tailored cuff with chrome stud light, red fog over a stage floor, a gold free-show fact chip on a black invitation card, and a clean crowd/spotlight abstraction. The design should feel premium and composed, not chaotic, while still carrying restrained danger.

## 5. Composition notes

- Use four proof modules.
- Keep each proof label to two or three words.
- Place headline in a top band with high contrast.
- Put the proof grid in the center, with generous black negative space.
- Add CTA and safety note in lower safe zone.
- Use original illustration, approved generated-and-reviewed imagery, or designer-created abstract details.
- Avoid internal controls, cue timing, private production details, or unverifiable promises.

## 7. Guardrails and verification

- Substantiate all claims as sensory/event-positioning language, not guarantees.
- Label any generated imagery as needed by final publishing policy.
- Do not imply that a specific effect, song, cue, performer action, or crowd size is guaranteed.
- Use no unapproved likenesses, protected creative references, official marks, or exact makeup/costume replication.
- Include safety language when using fog/flashing/light language.
- Reconfirm platform dimensions and policies before export.

## 8. Output request

Create three original interest-stage compositions: one square proof grid, one 4:5 vertical proof poster, and one LinkedIn/landscape credibility card. Show four clear proof points using the polished editorial grid style. Deliver editable text zones, text-free alternate, alt-text draft, and filename pattern.

## 9. Alt-text draft

A black, chrome, and red-gold promotional proof-grid for Just One KISS with four polished cards describing the theatrical tribute event's invitation style, stage atmosphere, free-show clarity, and fan-night energy.
