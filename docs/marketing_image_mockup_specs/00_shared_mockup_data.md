## 3. Campaign voice and visible-copy rules

- Voice: refined arena confidence; theatrical but controlled; direct, polished, and credible.
- Core metaphor: the beast is civilized for the invitation but still unmistakably dangerous under the suit.
- Headline rhythm: short, all-caps, declarative, no more than two beats per asset.
- Fact chips: compact verified facts only.
- CTA grammar: `Save July 25`, `Join updates`, `Plan your night`, `Get directions`, `Tell your crew`, `Ask a question`.
- Forbidden conversion language: buy, purchase, checkout, tickets on sale, VIP, limited seats, low stock, final chance, official, authorized, endorsed.
- Safety/disclaimer tone: plain, compact, not jokey.
- Required relationship posture when space allows: independent theatrical rock tribute; no outside partnership claimed or implied.

## 6. Crop and safe-zone behavior

- Square and 4:5: keep `Just One KISS`, the primary headline, July 25, and the CTA/fact chip cluster inside the central 70%.
- 9:16: protect top and bottom interface zones; keep headline in upper-middle, facts in center cards, CTA above lower safe zone.
- 1.91:1: use a composed left text block and right hero/symbol field; move secondary facts into a lower chrome strip.
- 300x250: use only `Just One KISS`, `July 25`, `Free show`, `Interlochen / US 31`, and one CTA.
- Header crops: preserve negative space for platform overlays and avoid edge-locked text.
- Print: include tested QR/short URL placeholders, bleed, safe margins, readable safety and independent-tribute text.
- Multi-crop families: recompose typography and fact zones for each aspect ratio; do not center-crop the master.
