# Social Media Image Ad Specs – Master Cheat Sheet

For each platform, we list the **recommended image ad formats** (dimensions, aspect ratios, file types/sizes) based on official and authoritative sources. This “inventory” covers still-image ad options so your marketing team can plan the needed creative formats per campaign.

## Facebook (Meta) Ads  
Facebook feed ads support square or vertical images. **Recommended dimensions:** *1440×1440 px* (1:1) or *1440×1800 px* (4:5); minimum 600×600 px.  These ensure full-screen display on mobile (vertical images only deliver on mobile).  For **Stories ads**, use *1440×2560 px* (9:16). Common file types are JPG/PNG (GIFs only for some placements) and max size ≈30 MB.  Other FB ad image specs include: Right-Column (desktop-only) ads – at least 1080×1080 px (1:1); Marketplace ads – 1080×1080 px; Carousel cards – 1080×1080 px each. In all cases, keep key content centered (Meta recommends leaving ~14% margins on Stories/Reels).  

- **News Feed (image ad):** 1440×1440 (1:1) or 1440×1800 (4:5); min 600×600. JPG/PNG up to 30 MB. Text limit: headline ≤40 chars.  
- **Stories (image ad):** 1440×2560 (9:16). Leave ~250 px top and 340 px bottom safe zones. JPG/PNG up to 30 MB.  
- **Right Column (desktop):** 1080×1080 (1:1) recommended (min 254×133). JPG/PNG up to 30 MB.  
- **Marketplace/Search ads:** 1080×1080 (1:1) recommended (min 600×600). JPG/PNG up to 30 MB.  
- **Carousel ads:** 1080×1080 each (all cards same aspect). Up to 10 images per ad; JPG/PNG ≤30 MB each.

## Instagram Ads  
Instagram feed supports horizontal, square or vertical images. **Recommended:** *1080×1080 px* (1:1, square) or *1080×1350 px* (4:5, vertical) or *1080×566 px* (1.91:1, landscape). In practice, an image 1080 px wide (height 566–1350) will display fully without cropping. Instagram Stories/Reels ads use *1080×1920 px* (9:16); keep critical content out of the top/bottom 14–20% of the frame to avoid overlays. File types: JPG/PNG up to 30 MB. Note that some guides suggest up to 1440×1440 for square ads, but Instagram will downscale any larger image to 1080 px width, so 1080×1080 is sufficient.

- **Feed Image (square):** 1080×1080 px (min width 500 px). Aspect 1:1. JPG/PNG ≤30 MB.  
- **Feed Image (vertical):** 1080×1350 px (4:5). Good for mobile; will crop to ~1080×1440 in grid view.  
- **Feed Image (horizontal):** 1080×566 px (1.91:1). Aspect 1.91:1.  
- **Stories Ad:** 1080×1920 px (9:16). Safe zone ~1080×1610.  
- **Reels Ad (vertical):** 1080×1920 px (9:16). Keep logo/text within central 1080×1350.

## LinkedIn Ads  
LinkedIn sponsored single-image ads (Feed/Sponsored Content) support landscape, square, and vertical formats. **Official specs:** Landscape (1.91:1) *1200×628 px*, Square (1:1) *1200×1200 px*. A vertical 4:5 *720×900 px* image is allowed on mobile only. Minimums are low (360×360 min) and max resolution is very high (7680×4320). File: JPG/PNG/GIF up to 5 MB. Text copy limits (headline ≤70, intro ≤150 chars) are also published. 

- **Single Image Ad (landscape):** 1200×628 px (1.91:1). Delivers on desktop/mobile.  
- **Single Image Ad (square):** 1200×1200 px (1:1). Recommended for feed.  
- **Single Image Ad (vertical, mobile-only):** 720×900 px (4:5). Recommended over 1.91:1 to avoid letterboxing on mobile.  
- **Carousel Ad:** 1080×1080 px each (1:1). 2–10 cards, max 10 MB each. Use same ratio across all cards.  
- **File:** JPG/PNG/GIF up to 5 MB.

## X (Twitter) Ads  
X (formerly Twitter) ads support square and wide images. **Recommended:** *1200×1200 px* (1:1) or *1200×628 px* (≈1.91:1) for standalone image ads. In practice, X accepts many ratios (1:1, 1.91:1, 16:9, 9:16, 4:5, 2:3). For **Website Card** or certain ad cards, use *800×418 px* (1.91:1) or *800×800 px* (1:1). File: JPG/PNG (WebP now supported too) under 5 MB. Note X no longer supports GIF in ads. 

- **Standalone Image Ad (in-tweet):** 1200×1200 px (1:1) or 1200×628 px (1.91:1). JPG/PNG, ≤5 MB.  
- **Carousel Ad:** 800×800 px (1:1) or 800×418 px (1.91:1). All cards must share the same aspect ratio. JPG/PNG.  
- **Website Card Image:** 800×418 px (1.91:1) recommended.  
- **In-Feed Image (any ratio):** Hootsuite notes *recommended 1600×900 px* (1.91:1) for general posts, but ads should use the specific ad sizes above.  
- **Profile/Header (branding, not ad):** Profile 400×400 px circle; Header 1500×500 px (3:1).

## Google Ads (Display & Discovery)  
Google Ads (especially Responsive Display and Performance Max) use both horizontal and square images. The official help states the **most common sizes are 1200×628 px** (approx 1.91:1) and **1200×1200 px** (1:1).  These cover most placements (Display, Gmail, Discover). Google also supports: vertical 4:5 (e.g. 960×1200) and portrait 9:16 (1080×1920) for feeds and Stories placements. File types: JPG/PNG (GIF also for web banners); file size typically max 5 MB for images (banner ads often require ≤150 KB, e.g. a 300×250 ad). Common static banner sizes (for traditional Display ads) include Medium Rectangle *300×250*, Leaderboard *728×90*, Mobile Banner *320×50*, etc. Use high-res (1200 px+) for responsive ads, letting Google scale as needed.

- **Landscape (Display/Discovery):** 1200×628 px (1.91:1) recommended. Min 600×314. JPG/PNG up to 5 MB.  
- **Square:** 1200×1200 px (1:1) recommended. JPG/PNG up to 5 MB.  
- **Portrait (4:5):** 960×1200 px recommended (mobile-only). JPG/PNG up to 5 MB.  
- **Story (9:16):** 1080×1920 px (for YouTube/Discover feeds).  
- **Banner (300×250):** 300×250 px (1.2:1) common display ad size. File (JPG/PNG/GIF) max ~150 KB.  

## Bluesky Ads/Posts  
Bluesky posts (image tweets) allow up to 4 images. **Recommended sizes:** Square *1080×1080 px*; Landscape *1200×627 px* (≈1.91:1); Portrait *627×1200 px* (4:5). Link previews (if posting a URL) use *1200×630 px* by default. Profile photos are 400×400 px (displayed circular); header images 1500×500 px (3:1). File type: JPG/PNG up to 2 MB. These specs align with community guides and ensure images appear without unexpected cropping.

- **Post Image (square):** 1080×1080 px (1:1).  
- **Post Image (landscape):** 1200×627 px (1.91:1).  
- **Post Image (portrait):** 627×1200 px (4:5).  
- **Link Preview Image:** 1200×630 px.  
- **Profile Photo:** 400×400 px (circle).  
- **Header (Cover) Photo:** 1500×500 px (3:1).

**Sources:** Official ad guides and up-to-date spec summaries from Media Cheat Sheet and reputable social media marketing blogs (Hootsuite, SocialPilot) have been consulted. Where discrepancies exist (e.g. Instagram square posts 1080×1080 vs 1440×1440), we note recommended resolutions that work in all cases. Citations above link to the platforms’ own help or authoritative cheat-sheets, ensuring the specs here are current and accurate.
