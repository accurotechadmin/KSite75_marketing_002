# Facebook / Meta Still-Image Ad Prompts


## Source basis

This prompt set synthesizes repository-controlled campaign, brand, public-copy, image-policy, and platform-planning guidance. It is designed for future copy/paste use in fresh image-generation sessions. The prompts are self-contained by design, but the human operator should still upload the current website screenshot when using them.

External platform specs can change; confirm exact paid-media requirements immediately before final spend/export. Where a platform does not offer conventional paid still-image ads, treat the prompts as organic, profile, community, or still-image-adjacent creative until verified paid placement rules exist.


## Shared prompt rules for every prompt in this document

Use the uploaded website screenshot as the primary visual reference for the current Just One KISS look: near-black page field, red/amber smoke glow, huge compressed white all-caps headline type, yellow/gold fact chips, red-orange CTA pills, chrome/gold borders, rounded black cards, road-case texture, stage truss geometry, and practical utility text. You may invent new depictions and compositions, but they must remain true to the existing brand story and style.

Project facts to preserve as readable real text when the placement has enough room: `Just One KISS`; `Free show`; `July 25, 2026`; `Cycle Moore Legacy`; `Interlochen / US 31`; `No ticket required`; `RSVP appreciated`; `Get the July 25 drop`. For practical-detail assets, use `11075 US 31 South, Interlochen, MI 49643`, `Camping $10/night; regular Cycle Moore charges apply for longer stays`, and a compact safety note: `Loud sound, bright lights, fog, and flashing/strobe-style looks may be used.`

Rights/safety guardrails: create original public-ready theatrical rock tribute imagery only. Do not use official KISS logos, official album art, official photos, exact protected makeup, official costumes copied exactly, restricted fonts, platform logos, celebrity likeness replication, or any implication of official KISS/Gene Simmons sponsorship, authorization, partnership, or endorsement. Do not use paid-ticket language such as buy, purchase, checkout, VIP, tickets on sale, limited seats, or scarcity language implying a transaction. Do not invent venue, traffic, weather, security, camping, accessibility, or parking claims. Treat the asset as `GEN` / general campaign marketing, not a precise show-cue asset.

Campaign story sequence to express across the requested image: Awareness = the night exists; Interest = the ritual and spectacle; Consideration = practical arrival confidence; Conversion = update-list / RSVP without purchase; Retention = tell your crew / open the road case. Keep the selected piece readable, crop-safe, and platform-native.

### Prompt — Feed square awareness

Copy/paste this whole prompt into the image-generation LLM session:

```text
Create one still-image marketing ad for Just One KISS.

Placement and size: Feed square awareness; final canvas 1440x1440 px, 1:1.

First, study the uploaded screenshot of the current Just One KISS website. Match its black/chrome/fire event-portal style: near-black background, red/amber smoke, chrome/gold edges, huge compressed white arena-poster typography, yellow fact chips, red-orange CTA button language, rounded black cards, road-case/stage-truss texture, and practical information presented as real readable text. You may invent a new depiction and composition, but it must look like it belongs to the same world as the screenshot.

Campaign stage: Awareness / The Night Exists.

Required visible copy / lockup: `YOU WANTED THE BEST?` + `JULY 25 IS COMING.` + `Free show · July 25, 2026 · Cycle Moore Legacy · Interlochen / US 31`; CTA chip `Save July 25` or `Join updates`.

Art direction: Black arena void with huge white compressed headline, original chrome shards or truss geometry, red/orange smoke along the bottom, distant stage-light flare, and only an original rim-lit silhouette or shadow form.

Crop and layout instructions: Center the headline in the central 70%; chips in lower third; CTA pill at bottom; keep enough negative space for mobile feed cropping.

Global facts and guardrails: Just One KISS is an independent theatrical rock tribute event inspired by Gene Simmons/KISS-style spectacle. It is a free show on July 25, 2026 at Cycle Moore Legacy, Interlochen / US 31. No ticket is required; RSVP/update-list signup is appreciated. Keep facts readable when included. Include compact safety/disclaimer text only where the layout has enough room: loud sound, bright lights, fog, and flashing/strobe-style looks may be used; independent theatrical rock tribute, no outside partnership claimed or implied.

Do not create official KISS logos, exact protected makeup, album art, official photos, official costumes copied exactly, restricted fonts, platform logos, celebrity-likeness replication, or any sponsor/authorization implication. Do not use paid-ticket language such as buy, purchase, checkout, tickets on sale, VIP, limited seats, or scarcity language implying a transaction. Do not invent venue operations, traffic, weather, security, accessibility, camping, or parking claims.

Output a polished, production-ready still image with no extra explanation.
```
